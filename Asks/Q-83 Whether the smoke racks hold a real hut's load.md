# Whether the smoke racks hold a real hut's load (Q-83)

*open · Zach*

## The question
smokehouse_capacity.md (22 Nov): the racks hold about 40 kg on 3 spaces (about 2.7 kg a m2), a tenth to a twentieth of a real hut; 3 nights is about a month real, two to three times a keeping smoke. Proposed: 250 fish a space, the fitting 2 spaces holding 500; 1 night a lot; 1 unit to hang 30 fish; 1 firewood per 60 fish; tending 1 unit a night (a named departure). Needs the fitting's own holds (keeping.py). The hearth rack's 3 nights need the same check.
