## The question
Zach, 20 Nov: 'this isn't necessarily a ceiling just for the medieval, it might be a much higher technological requirements. Medieval can have lower ceiling, but still not too low'; '4. idk bout that'. The draft's high ceilings (people fed per food worker: arable 5 to 8, the runs 6 to 15, a master hunter 4 to 8) become the later-technology ceilings; the medieval worlds take a lower band, enough for specialists to show within a run

**Recommended:** medieval ceilings about half the draft's (arable 3 to 5, herding 3 to 4, a master hunter 3 to 5, resident fishing 3 to 4, the runs 4 to 8 in a good year), measured first by the bots, then set

**Default:** the bots measure what the world gives before any band is fixed
