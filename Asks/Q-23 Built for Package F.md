# Q-23 · Built for Package F

*open · 37.4, Zach to confirm (resources, fuel: 37.2)*

## The question
Built for Package F (24 Oct), choices the spec left open, for Zach to confirm or change
1. an older world's `wood` is read as its trees, with young growth and dead wood in a copse's proportions; scrub with no wood state gets scrub's (so old worlds' scrub now gives firewood)
2. a forest turns to scrub under 40% of its trees and back above 45%; felling is done only on forest, so a wood's last 40% cannot be felled
3. gathering gives 3 x the square root of how full the dead wood is; the pool's capacity rises with brash
4. a gale throws 0.2% of the trees as windfall
5. a house's floor is its size; each hearth or ring burns its heat; a house too big for its hearths is warmed in proportion (between housed and unwarmed); a fire ring alone 1.15 in the cold
6. a fire in the open is shared by those sleeping out at one node, or one party on the road, those who chose a fire first
7. food that wants a fire is eaten raw after all that needs none; fresh meat now counts as better cooked (1.5, raw 0.8)
8. axes are heard from any felling on a parcel
9. children may be set to gathering firewood
10. not built: game and forage reading the forest (cover, browse, light), drought and forest fire, wet timber.
