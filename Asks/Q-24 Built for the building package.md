2. camps keep no floor and no store limit, taking 4 spaces of ground on built ground or scrub
3. an upper storey costs the room alone x1.5 or x2, without the added-room quarter off as well
4. repair "for every 4 beds of the building's size" read as every 4 spaces of floor
5. a mason is anyone who knows a mason's craft (no masonry technique), and a mason's fitting may be built by any mason

7. not built as written: the woodshed's "kept dry" (the engine has no wet timber in stores, [[Q-23 Built for Package F|Q-23]] (10))
8. seeded buildings get no free floor unless the world file gives `free:`, so some houses are full on day 1 (combe's Hobb House): should world files give free space, or seeding a default
9. warmth: a hearth's 12 spaces set against the floor in spaces ([[D2-159 Shelter and fire are separate|D2-159]]), not the old size in people
