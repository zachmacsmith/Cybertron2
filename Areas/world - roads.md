# world/roads

*Roads: stretches, landmarks, crossing and meetings* · the spec: [[Roads stretches, landmarks, crossing and meetings|Roads: stretches, landmarks, crossing and meetings]]

> A road (an edge) is one place in the game with parts inside it, as a node has parcels. It has one stretch for each day of its length and, on some stretches, a landmark: a ford, a bridge, a pass, a narrows, a spring. A road with no landmarks behaves as one terrain throughout, as before.
