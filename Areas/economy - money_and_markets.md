# economy/money_and_markets

*Money and markets* · the spec: [[Money and markets|Money and markets]]

> Goods change hands at a node through offers that settle both sides at once when accepted, at no cost in work units. An offer goes to one person, or to anyone at the node while the offerer stays. One leg can be a promise, which makes a debt that exists only as information. Prices are what agents saw
