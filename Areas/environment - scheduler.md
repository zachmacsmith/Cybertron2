# environment/scheduler

*The scheduler: external change* · the spec: [[The scheduler external change|The scheduler: external change]]

> External change is drawn from the seed by one central scheduler, the same in every arm of an experiment, and kept apart from the internal consequences of what people do. It runs in layers (eras, years, spells, incidents, spreads), each setting the odds of the next. Every draw is keyed by kind, place
