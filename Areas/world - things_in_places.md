# world/things_in_places

*Things in places: putting down, hiding, the dead, burial* · the spec: [[Things in places putting down, hiding, the dead, burial|Things in places: putting down, hiding, the dead, burial]]

> Anything not in someone's hands or a building's store lies at a spot: a parcel at a node, or a stretch of road. Goods put down, goods hidden, a dropped load, remains of the dead, a camp, traces. Whoever finds a thing can take it. Things in the open spoil much faster, and bodies and rotting food rot
