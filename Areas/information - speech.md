*Speech at a node* · the spec: [[Speech at a node|Speech at a node]]

> People speak to one person, in a building, or to the whole town. Only speech to one person calls them away from their day: they hear it before work and may answer or change their plans. Speech in a building or to the town is heard by everyone it reaches, without interrupting them.
