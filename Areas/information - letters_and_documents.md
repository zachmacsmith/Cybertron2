# information/letters_and_documents

*Letters and documents* · the spec: [[Letters and documents|Letters and documents]]

> Information crosses between nodes only by being carried. Rumour travels free with whoever goes; letters, and later papers that are centralised and delivered, cost something to make and to carry. A document is a good: written on parchment, it has a place and can be carried, stored, stolen and lost.
