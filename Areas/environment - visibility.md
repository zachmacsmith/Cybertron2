# environment/visibility

*Visibility: what a place and the day let the senses reach* · the spec: [[Visibility what a place and the day let the senses reach|Visibility: what a place and the day let the senses reach]]

> Every place has a visibility for each sense: how much it lets through. It is not stored: it is computed each day from the environment (the land's cover and state, smoke, what is making noise, the weather), and multiplies every sign of that sense noticed there. Light enters only for what happens at n
