# world/places

*Places: nodes and sites* · the spec: [[Places nodes and sites|Places: nodes and sites]]

> A node is a village, a valley or a camp: the people there and the land they work. The places used every day are sites inside a node, used without travelling. If you could go there and be back before nightfall, it is the same node.
