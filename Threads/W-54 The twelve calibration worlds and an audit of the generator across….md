# The twelve calibration worlds and an audit of the generator across… (W-54)

*active · 37.2 · touched 20 Nov (3 days ago)*

> **Next:** Zach on the audit's faults (which to fix, each remakes every world); climate from the land ([[Q-56 How the weather should follow each place's climate from the generated…|Q-56]]); then rerun the suite

## What it is
The calibration worlds ([[D2-228 Calibrate across a distribution of worlds|D2-228]]) and a generator audit across them

## Done so far, and what is left
built and on main 20 Nov (merged with [[W-62 Merging the water work from its twelve branches into main|W-62]]): twelve worlds at two seeds, all 24 generate; expectations from real places (approximate: the sources were recalled, not fetched); the suite report (02zzt) with ranges, orderings, collapse checks, nutrition, wildlife, weather and environments; one generator fix (a laid lake kept outside the lake cap). Results: the generator's climate 43 of 48 in range, the engine's weather 142 of 216 (the known gap: it reads only height), orderings 15 of 16 by the generator and 3 of 13 by the engine, no collapse; the brown bear rises to 4 to 6 times its reference; gales, frosts and rain between years too many or too wide; rivers low 40% of the year. The audit's 11 faults (02zzt): the water allocator (at most two cells), no coast or fen villages, the rain sweep, chalk everywhere, climate read only at the tree line, uplands too high, a fixed 0.3 wood share, too little wet ground, a laid lake's size by luck, upland lakes too many, arable only on floodplain clay
