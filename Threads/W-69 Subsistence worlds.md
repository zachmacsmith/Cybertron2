## What it is
Zach, 20 Nov ([[D2-242 Subsistence test worlds|D2-242]]): the runs world did not test whether a village can live off runs alone (it has fields; fish looks badly calibrated). Build a set of calibration worlds: runs only, hunting only; each survivable at medium skill and capital, with a surplus up to a bound at more. Folded in: fish nutrition (the eel at a fifth of its real food, [[W-64 Shared units for food, weight and work|W-64]]), the run numbers, the river frequencies ([[Q-52 Environment systems|Q-52]] 6), the brook fished out ([[Q-57 Fishing, from the lake run|Q-57]] c), hunting against farming ([[Q-46 Brief 4c as built|Q-46]] b, c; [[W-22 Hunting against farming and gathering in generated worlds|W-22]]'s measured changes)
