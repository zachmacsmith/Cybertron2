# D4-118 · The land generator takes a designed world

*live · 13 Nov · spec: [[world - generation|world/generation]]*

## The rule
(proposals/peoples_benchmark.md, retrospective): places pinned by position (`at`, `within`), soil (rich, fair, poor: set on the land when the site lacks it), resources and water; `vacant: true` places with land laid out by a `land` word (open, wood, waste, rock), sized by `room`, with a one-cell clearing to build on, and no houses, fields, herds or people; `roads:` builds only the roads named; `warn_only` takes a check off the required list
