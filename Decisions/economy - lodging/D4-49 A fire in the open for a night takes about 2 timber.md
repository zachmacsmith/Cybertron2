# A fire in the open for a night takes about 2 timber (D4-49)

*live · 23 Oct · spec: [[economy - lodging|economy/lodging]]*

## The rule
**A fire in the open for a night takes about 2 timber; a camp takes 3 timber to build and 1 timber a round to keep lit**

## Where it came from
conversation ("is 0.5 timber being better than a house weird? Surely it should need at least like 2 timber as a temporary? Then they can build one a camp with 3, and keep it lit wirh 1 timber each round")

## What it replaced
no fire in the open; a camp's fire at a house's rate (engine as built)
