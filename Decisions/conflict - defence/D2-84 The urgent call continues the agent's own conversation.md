# D2-84 · The urgent call continues the agent's own conversation

*live · 23 Oct · spec: [[conflict - defence|conflict/defence]], [[conflict - fuse_events|conflict/fuse_events]], [[core - round|core/round]], [[information - prompts|information/prompts]]*

## The rule
**The urgent call continues the agent's own conversation:** it is appended to the agent's context as it stands (that day's turn and what it carries from the days before, [[D4-27 Conversation memory|D4-27]]), so it is the same agent deciding; it shows what the agent sees of the event, what it holds, who is with it, and the event's menu

## Where it came from
conversation ("otherwise it's not really the same agent")

## What it replaced
a fresh short prompt for the urgent call
