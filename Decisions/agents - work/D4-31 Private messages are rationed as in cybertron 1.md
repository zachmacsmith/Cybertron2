# Private messages are rationed as in cybertron 1

- **state**: live
- **source**: conversation ("port v1's private message ration, and make public speech use one action"); 38.14 §8
- **replaces_text**: M1's unrationed `tell` and free `say`

## Text
**Private messages are rationed as in cybertron 1** (a daily number: 3 plus a personal jitter drawn once, mostly 3-6, never above 10; answers count against it), and **speaking aloud (to the town or in a building) takes one unit of the day's work**
