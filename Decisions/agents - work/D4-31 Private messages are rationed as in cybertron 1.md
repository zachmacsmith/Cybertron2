# D4-31 · Private messages are rationed as in cybertron 1

*live · 23 Oct · spec: [[agents - work|agents/work]], [[information - speech|information/speech]]*

## The rule
(a daily number: 3 plus a personal jitter drawn once, mostly 3-6, never above 10; answers count against it), and **speaking aloud (to the town or in a building) takes one unit of the day's work**

## Where it came from
conversation ("port v1's private message ration, and make public speech use one action"); 38.14 §8

## What it replaced
M1's unrationed `tell` and free `say`
