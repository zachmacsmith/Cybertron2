# Every sign and every event is noticed by the one noticing rule

- **state**: live
- **source**: conversation ("every sign should follow the same rules Why would you ever build onethat doesn't?"; "do events not have locations as well?"; "a location could span maybe a whole node, or just a single parcel, or an edge, or jsut a portion of an edge, it dpenedns on what it is for the event or state that is being experience"); proposals/one_noticing_rule.md (retrospective)
- **replaces_text**: the older bundles (crops, fields, pastures, wild land, herds, buildings, people, the sky) and events read by salience x relevance wherever one was

## Text
**Every sign and every event is noticed by the one noticing rule** (own conspicuousness x visibility x presence x attention x the eye), none by a formula of its own; **how far each reaches depends on what it is:** the whole node (the weather, a death, a fire, an alarm, a fight), a parcel (a crop, a herd, work, a trade, a gate opened), a building (those inside, and its parcel), a stretch of road or the whole road
