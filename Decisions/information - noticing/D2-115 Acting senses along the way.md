# D2-115 · Acting senses along the way

*live · 24 Oct · spec: [[information - noticing|information/noticing]], [[world - parcel_graph|world/parcel_graph]]*

## The rule
**Acting senses along the way:** whoever acts on a parcel senses the parcels it is in, those next to them, and those it must pass through to get there, without choosing a route
