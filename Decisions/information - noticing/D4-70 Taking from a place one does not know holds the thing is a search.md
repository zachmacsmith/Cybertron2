# Taking from a place one does not know holds the thing is a search (D4-70)

*live · 2 Nov · spec: [[information - noticing|information/noticing]]*

## The rule
**Taking from a place one does not know holds the thing is a search:** each thing there holding what one asks for that one did not know of is found by a chance from how plain it lies and how it is hidden (own conspicuousness and hiding x the parcel's visibility x a search's attention x one's eye): a fresh heap or pack in the open almost surely, something under brush often, something well buried seldom; found, it is known from a close look. A refusal says one looked and found none. Slaughter says where its meat and hide went (into one's hands, or a store)

## Where it came from
conversation ("If they try to pick something up in a place, they must know that it is there, so if they don't then there should at least be a check for how hidden / visible it is ... if it is just slaughtered meat they will almost certainly find it, and if it is hidden there is still a chance they find it"); proposals/search_on_take.md (retrospective)

## What it replaced
a take from a place refused unless the thing had been noticed before
