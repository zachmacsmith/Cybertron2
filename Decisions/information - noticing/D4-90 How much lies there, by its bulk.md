# How much lies there, by its bulk (D4-90)

*live · 2 Nov · spec: [[information - noticing|information/noticing]]*

## The rule
**How much lies there, by its bulk:** goods in the open are more conspicuous the more there are (1 − exp(−w/4.34)); from afar a heap by size, on a close look each good within a band, attending (to its tag or parcel) a count; food spoiling in the open signs by bulk (a stronger stench, a musty smell, rats)

## Where it came from
conversation ("senses like that should scale with number"; "should say like a heap of around X to Y"; "attending to a heap should give more info"; "much larger stenches, or rats")

## What it replaced
heap 0.9 / pack 0.4 and "a heap of grain" with no amount
