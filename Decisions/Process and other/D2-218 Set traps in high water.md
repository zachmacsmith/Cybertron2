# Set traps in high water (D2-218)

*live · 16 Nov*

## The rule
(OPEN [[Q-55 The weir as built, measured|Q-55]] (4)): a flood wrecks a set trap in running water very likely but not always (calibrate, about 0.85; a spate half the time, 37.4's reading, kept unless Zach says otherwise), the trap's catch lost with it; mending a weir section costs about 6% of its build a level; an open weir takes 0.1 of its shut share; how the river's flow level is worked out is 37.2's to decide

## Where it came from
conversation (16 Nov: "I think shouldn't wreck a set trap always, just very likely. Open weir and mending sound ok. Flow level seems like something you should be deciding")

## What it replaced
a flood always wrecking a set trap
