# A water-only village is made able to last the year by (D2-348)

*live · 23 Nov*

## The rule
**A water-only village is made able to last the year by (a) a spring calendar (a lamprey run, coarse fish spawning in the shallows in spring) and (b) resident fish and shallows big enough to matter, at their best about a quarter of the village ([[D2-316 A runs world means a water-only world|D2-316]] still holds), built in stage 5b before live run 2.**
