# D4-54 · Going home at dusk is a chance, not a rule

*live · 24 Oct*

## The rule
**Going home at dusk is a chance, not a rule:** a herd that wandered walks home at dusk more likely if its cows are in milk, less likely the better it is feeding where it stands, so it often stays the night where the food is; a hungry herd stays out

## Where it came from
conversation ("do herds always return at dusk? I feel like they should have a chance, or be more liekly to just end up in a parcel with food and stay there?"); proposals/herds_at_dusk.md (retrospective)

## What it replaced
cows in milk always walked home at dusk; other herds never did
