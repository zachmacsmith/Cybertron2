# Roads are mostly a day long (D2-182)

*live · 3 Nov*

## The rule
**Roads are mostly a day long:** the generator joins neighbouring places by roads of about one day; a two-day road is rare (where the land gives nowhere to stop: a moor, a pass); a longer journey passes through places on the way ([[D4-10 Both kinds of distance|D4-10]]'s waypoints), so a route of three days is three roads; where a day's road would cross wild land with no settlement, the generator puts a stopping place there (a hamlet where the site allows, else a wild stop at a spring, a ford or a meadow: proposed); the default region is about 50 km square for 4 settlements (amends [[D2-181 (Roads and region amended by D2-182.)|D2-181]])

## Where it came from
conversation ("most roads should be more like 1 day. 3 days for a road should be less common, and would usually pass through a place along the way")
