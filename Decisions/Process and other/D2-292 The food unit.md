# The food unit (D2-292)

*live · 22 Nov*

## The rule
**The food unit: 1 food = 2,500 kcal; an adult eats 1 a night, a child 0.6, an elder 0.8, more for hard work, cold and nights out (the default taken on [[Q-69 The basis for food and work|Q-69]], now decided).**
