# (Roads and region amended by D2-182.) (D2-181)

*live · 3 Nov*

## The rule
**The generator's defaults** (all world settings): cells about 150 m apart (about 2 ha, a unit of size); a region about 70 km square with 4 settlements, at least a day's walk apart (roads of 1 to 3 days, a road day about 25 to 30 km); each settlement's land sized by its capacity target ([[D2-161 Seed nodes below what they sustain|D2-161]]), not by its reach, with wild land between settlements; fields within the ring the target sets; wild nodes for areas of at least about 10 km² more than half a day out; 6 to 15 parcels a node; macro cells of about 5 to 10 km. Checked against the earlier designs (generator_stage1.md §10)

## Where it came from
conversation ("sure. As long as you compare these to the original reasonings behind the old designs and things still check out, then keep this")

## What it replaced
the 30 km region and 2 km field ring first proposed
