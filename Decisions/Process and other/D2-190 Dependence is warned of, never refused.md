# D2-190 · Dependence is warned of, never refused

*live · 2 Nov*

## The rule
**Dependence is warned of, never refused:** a world's economic checks (02zl) refuse only a run its food or heat cannot cover even counting all it holds; a village that must bring in food or fuel is a warning (the carrying burden, marked high above 0.12), as some food specialisation and dependence matter, more so as societies grow

## Where it came from
conversation ("This should be a warning not a blocker")

## What it replaced
the carrying burden as a refusal (02zl first draft)
