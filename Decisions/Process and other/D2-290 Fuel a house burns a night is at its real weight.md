# Fuel a house burns a night is at its real weight (D2-290)

*live · 22 Nov*

## The rule
**Fuel a house burns a night is at its real weight: about 10 kg (one bundle) a normal night, about 30 kg a cold one. The kinds of fuel (firewood, timber, charcoal) and their heats are revisited and fleshed out with the timber economy ([[W-76 The timber economy|W-76]]).**
