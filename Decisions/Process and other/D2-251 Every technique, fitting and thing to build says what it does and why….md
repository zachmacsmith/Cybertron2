# Every technique, fitting and thing to build says what it does and why… (D2-251)

*live · 20 Nov*

## The rule
**Every technique, fitting and thing to build says what it does and why it is worth it, in a ladder of detail by familiarity: a short sentence for one who has only heard of it, a longer one with what it gives back for one who knows it roughly, the specifics (inputs, rates, returns) for one who knows it well; one pattern for everything the engine describes [no spec]**

## Where it came from
conversation (20 Nov: "I think different techniques / things to build should come with a sentence describing what they do / are helpful for. Each should have a ladder of detail about its relevance based on familiarity ... I think this should be better solidified into a pattern for everything ... They can have a sentence saying what they do, vs specifics etc. that align with how familiar someone is with it")
