# Founders' seeded lines about other households follow where they live… (D2-349)

*live · 24 Nov*

## The rule
**Founders' seeded lines about other households follow where they live and what is known: households in one's own village get the neighbour lines; households in other villages one's household knows of get lines of their own kind (that village, not a neighbour's); villages one's household does not know of get no seeded lines. Built in stage 5b part 3.**

## Where it came from
conversation (24 Nov: "apparently the livelihood memory seeder assumes all families know of each other / live in the same village? It should treat households in other villages differently, and other villages that the household does not know of as different again (no seeded memories)")
