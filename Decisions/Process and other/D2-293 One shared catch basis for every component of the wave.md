# One shared catch basis for every component of the wave (D2-293)

*live · 22 Nov*

## The rule
**One shared catch basis for every component of the wave: the calibrated bot runs (the village's weir about 1,830 kg and traps about 740 kg over two autumns, peaks about 330 kg a night), checked against real eel-fishery records before building, so whether a household can bank a winter is a finding and not an artefact of an assumed catch.**
