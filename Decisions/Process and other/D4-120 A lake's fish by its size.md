# A lake's fish by its size (D4-120)

*live · 17 Nov*

## The rule
**A lake's fish by its size:** a village's largest water parcel on a lake holds a stock by the lake's area (calibrate: 7,500 a km²), regrowing by the `threshold` rule at a rate that gives its yearly production (calibrate: 0.4 of the stock) on the 32-day scale; the stock is not scaled by the short year, the flow is (proposals/catch_and_lakes.md)

## Where it came from
conversation ("it's a pretty big lake"; "the limit on fishing be the extraction not the store itself"; "change the capacity of the lake to make sense")

## What it replaced
K 8 a unit of parcel size for a lake (replaced by [[D2-229 One fish stock per water body|D2-229]], 17 Nov: one stock per water body, sized by the whole lake)
