# Acts on held things take the thing as their target, by tag or a known… (D2-305)

*live · 23 Nov*

## The rule
**Acts on held things take the thing as their target, by tag or a known name, said in each act; one's own things are shown with their tags; unnamed, an act touches only one's own ([[D2-304 An unnamed act on several of one's own things at a place|D2-304]] for several); another's thing only when named, and called what it is; a household's things count as every member's own (Zach's 21 Nov answer in [[Q-80 Whether acts on owned things default to one's own|Q-80]], recorded now).**
