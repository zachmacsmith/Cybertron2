# D4-93 · One room, one fire

*live · 2 Nov*

## The rule
**One room, one fire:** a house burns 1 heat a normal night and 4 a cold one for each hearth; a fire ring alone counts as one, and a ring beside a hearth adds nothing; the prompt shows that figure; an unlit fire's night line names the fuel it lacked ("not enough fuel in its store or its woodpile (it needed 1 heat: 2 firewood or 0.5 timber)"); a theft from goods at a door is told as one, not as slipping into the house

## Where it came from
conversation (wb2: Wat's hearth beside his ring doubled the fuel unseen; "no timber in its store" taught him a false rule; "slipped into the Hearne hut" for grain taken outside it; "yes" to the fixes)

## What it replaced
hearths + rings each burning; "no timber in its store"; the store's theft wording for a door pile
