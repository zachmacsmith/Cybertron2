# Familiarity and the interface (D2-352)

*live · 24 Nov*

## The rule
**Familiarity and the interface: aware is only knowing it is a possibility (the name, and who does it); once a person can do it (a rough idea or more, or it is in the set everyone knows) they are shown at least the minimum interface to call it (how it is said and its required arguments); costs, options and the recipe's detail grow with familiarity; the set everyone knows is shown in full. Replaces [[D2-351 What a person is shown follows what they have learnt|D2-351]]'s clause that every familiarity shows the full interface.**

## Where it came from
conversation (24 Nov: "Ok maybe aware doesn't need the full interface you took that and made it all extravagant. I meant that if they know it well enough to do it, then they should at least know the minimum interface to call it, that doesn't necessarily mean its costs etc. the point of aware is just vaguely aware that it is a possibility.")
