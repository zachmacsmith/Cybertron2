# Macro features are compositions of primitives (D2-188)

*live · 3 Nov*

## The rule
**Macro features are compositions of primitives:** sea, coast, landmass, inlet, strait, isthmus, ridge (with passes at its low points), massif, plateau, scarp, basin, valley, gorge, saddle, river, lake, fen, delta, estuary, confluence, and belts of geology and the prevailing wind, combined in order and roughened by noise; each exposes named anchors for pins; a library of named configurations built from them is data (generator_stage1.md §11k); configurations needing tides, water as a need, floods or water travel wait for those
