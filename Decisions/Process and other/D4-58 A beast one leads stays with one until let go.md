# D4-58 · A beast one leads stays with one until let go

*live · 24 Oct*

## The rule
**A beast one leads stays with one until let go:** a pack ox that arrives with its party stays led (with its leader, carrying, listed under what one has, not wandering) until its leader turns it out, sets off without it, or dies; then it is an ordinary herd known by memory and noticing
