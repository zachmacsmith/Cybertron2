# A shelf life is stated one of two ways (D2-336)

*live · 23 Nov*

## The rule
**A shelf life is stated one of two ways: 'keeps for d days' (preserved goods: about a quarter lost by then, [[D2-289 A shelf life 'keeps d days' becomes a nightly loss read as about a…|D2-289]]) or 'goes off after d days' (fresh, perishable food: about half gone by then, so fresh fish and meat go off quickly after a few nights, as before the rebuild); both on the keeping curve's nights.**
