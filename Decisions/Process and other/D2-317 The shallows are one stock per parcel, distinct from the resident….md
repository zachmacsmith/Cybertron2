# The shallows are one stock per parcel, distinct from the resident… (D2-317)

*live · 23 Nov*

## The rule
**The shallows are one stock per parcel, distinct from the resident fish of a whole water body (which a rod or line can reach across a larger lake); shallows fishing has its own signs, for the different creatures it takes.**
