# Seed nodes below what they sustain (D2-161)

*live · 24 Oct*

## The rule
**Seed nodes below what they sustain:** about 60 to 70% of the yearly flow of the scarcest resource, so growth (children) presses later; capacity checks count yearly flows, not stocks; a world's designed dependence in one resource at a time

## Where it came from
02za; conversation

## What it replaced
worlds seeded at the most people their stocks carry for a run
