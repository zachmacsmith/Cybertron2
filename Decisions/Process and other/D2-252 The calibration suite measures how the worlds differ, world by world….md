# The calibration suite measures how the worlds differ, world by world… (D2-252)

*live · 20 Nov*

## The rule
**The calibration suite measures how the worlds differ, world by world and side by side: the sources of nutrition (food per unit of work and per year by source: arable by soil, herding, gathering, hunting by skill, fishing, the runs, preserving), the wildlife (kinds present, densities, where), the weather and its likelihood (types, wet days, frost, snow, storms, spates, floods, low water, ice), and the environments (soils, water held and drainage, fertility, slopes, wet ground, floodplains, covers and ecosystems, the tree line) [no spec]**
