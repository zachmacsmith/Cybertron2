# Practice alone can bring a person to known in any craft (D2-326)

*live · 23 Nov*

## The rule
**Practice alone can bring a person to known in any craft (so new crafts can emerge through hard work), only more slowly; teaching is much more efficient; passing on maintains a craft across generations. This replaces the rule that a skilled craft's last lesson comes only from a teacher.**
