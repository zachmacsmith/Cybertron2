# The weir's cost for now, to calibrate in live runs, on a lowland river (D2-302)

*live · 23 Nov*

## The rule
**The weir's cost for now, to calibrate in live runs, on a lowland river: wattle first section 12 units, 20 timber (poles), 10 withies; each added section (up to 3) 6 units, 8 timber, 5 withies; timber first section 20 units, 40 timber; each added 10 units, 15 timber; wattle wears 1.0, timber 0.5; upkeep a year a quarter of the build (wattle), an eighth (timber); upgrading costs the difference; a beck 0.3 times these, a large river 2.5 times; one copy of the cost in the data.**
