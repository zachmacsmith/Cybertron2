# D2-178 · The macro layer is templates and noise

*live · 3 Nov*

## The rule
**The macro layer is templates and noise:** a coarse world (cells of about 5 to 10 km) built from parametric shapes (island, archipelago, peninsula, bay, range, lake district, basin, delta) blended with noise; the region played is a window on it, chosen by pinned macro features or by a deterministic scan of the macro world for the best fit; fine detail is made only where it is needed, keyed by place; plate tectonics flagged for later (OPEN [[L-39 Plate tectonics for the macro layer|L-39]])
