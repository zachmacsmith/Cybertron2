# Each morning a person is told what they ate the night before (D2-342)

*live · 23 Nov*

## The rule
**Each morning a person is told what they ate the night before: which goods and how much, from which store, whether by a rule (which one) or by the default order, and how well it met their hunger (full, short, still hungry); and what the household drew from its store for the meal, so a store going down at the meal is seen as eaten, not as vanished.**

## Where it came from
conversation (23 Nov: "people should be told in the morning what they ate and if it was according to a rule. They seem to always think things are disappearing: are they told as an eating sign what they eat and how well it satiated their hunger?")
