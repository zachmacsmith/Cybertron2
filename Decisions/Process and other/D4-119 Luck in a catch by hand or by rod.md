# D4-119 · Luck in a catch by hand or by rod

*live · 17 Nov*

## The rule
**Luck in a catch by hand or by rod:** a stock yield with `spread` draws each unit's catch about its expected value (the method's most × the stock's fullness × skill and tool), at most the stock above its refuge; `fish by hand` and `angle` spread 0.5; other stock yields unchanged (proposals/catch_and_lakes.md)

## Where it came from
conversation ("yes, add the small chance")

## What it replaced
a fixed amount a unit
