# Each depth of cure is its own good (D2-295)

*live · 22 Nov*

## The rule
**Each depth of cure is its own good (hot-smoked, smoked, hard-cured, salt and dried fish and meat), with its own weight, food a kg and shelf life; a lot in a processing container becomes the next good when its nights are counted.**
