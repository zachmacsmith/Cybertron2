# D2-202 · The wild kinds and the people draw the same stocks on a village's land

*live · 9 Nov*

## The rule
**The wild kinds and the people draw the same stocks on a village's land:** what the ecology's kinds eat there (mast, nuts, fruit, roots, browse, grass) is drawn from the parcels' stocks, and what people take or graze leaves less for the wild kinds
