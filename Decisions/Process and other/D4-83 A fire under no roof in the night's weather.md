# A fire under no roof in the night's weather (D4-83)

*live · 2 Nov*

## The rule
**A fire under no roof in the night's weather:** a camp's fire and a fire in the open burn more fuel in rain and wind (x1.5) and snow (x1.25, more with snow lying), and in a downpour or a gale may go out in the night (a half chance, a fire ring keeping it a little better): those by it are cooked for but lie cold till morning (the cold's toll, an infant's cold risk); a sound house's hearth is not touched

## Where it came from
conversation ("what if we have it that a fire can't be lit in a camp when it rains during the night or snows?"; then "yes implement the graded version"); proposals/fire_weather.md (retrospective)

## What it replaced
camp and open fires the same in all weather
