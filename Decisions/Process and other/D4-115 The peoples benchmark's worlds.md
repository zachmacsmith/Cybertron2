# The peoples benchmark's worlds (D4-115)

*live · 13 Nov*

## The rule
(proposals/peoples_benchmark.md; review 38.69): each model is a people; a people's aim is its living numbers less the other peoples' together; each people starts as about 10, almost all early adults, in a home village of its own; the world is much larger than the homes, its places designed by hand with land types and rare resources so that expanding and intensifying are both open; every home feeds its people comfortably when farmed well (the capacity ladder's "worked" about 2x its people); travel in whole days; long horizons; built on the ecology branch (the wild and water packs)

## Where it came from
conversation (Zach: "their goal is the total population of the same model type, minus the population of any other model types"; "significantly larger than the starting area ... further, nicher areas with rare resources"; "starting carrying capacity should comfortably support"; "good farming"; "all travel distances should be integer multiples"; "ecology branch")
