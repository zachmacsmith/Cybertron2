# Names from parts, descriptions by the model (D2-186)

*live · 3 Nov*

## The rule
**Names from parts, descriptions by the model:** settlements and landmarks are named from lists of name elements chosen by their facts (an ending by the site: -ford at a ford, -ley a woodland clearing, -mere by a lake; a first part from the land: Ash-, Stan-, Mar-), parcels by plain patterns (Long Field, the Common, Brook Meadow), by keyed draws, never repeating within a world; the style a world setting; descriptions stay model-written ([[D2-179 Descriptions|D2-179]])
