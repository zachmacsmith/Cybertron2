# Going back to one's set gear is the act 'tend' (D2-294)

*live · 22 Nov*

## The rule
**Going back to one's set gear is the act 'tend' (checking, emptying, re-setting, taking up), for traps, snares, nets and lines alike; lift, check, empty, walk and collect are read as tend.**
