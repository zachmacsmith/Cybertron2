# What a person is shown follows what they have learnt (D2-351)

*live · 24 Nov*

## The rule
**What a person is shown follows what they have learnt: an act or technique is shown once they are familiar with it at all, and a defined set that everyone knows (the acts of daily life, family and society, and the basic crafts) is shown to everyone with its full description. Whatever is shown carries its full interface (how it is said, its arguments and every option, its cost, what it needs), at any familiarity; familiarity varies only the know-how (inputs and batch, what a tool does, the chance of success, the finer ways). Refines [[D2-350 Every act and every technique a person can take is registered with…|D2-350]].**
