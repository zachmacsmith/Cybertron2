# D2-191 · The generator designs within one envelope

*live · 2 Nov*

## The rule
(temperate_landscapes.md): seven landscape systems (river country, scarp and vale, sand country, upland, fen, coast, wildwood); ecosystems by each system's palette; hollows breached so land drains, lakes only in fens and uplands; land cleared by how long settled (`settled:` frontier 0.6, settled 0.3, long settled 0.15 of the land in wood); deep forest the core of any large wood; every configuration checked to make its own landscape; delta and rift wait outside the envelope

## Where it came from
conversation ("build this out")

## What it replaced
one terrain mixing landform, vegetation and distance; lakes in every hollow; forest wherever loam or clay
