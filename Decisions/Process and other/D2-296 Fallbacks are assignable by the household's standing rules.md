# Fallbacks are assignable by the household's standing rules (D2-296)

*live · 22 Nov*

## The rule
**Fallbacks are assignable by the household's standing rules: the head divides the household's spare units out as chores (who does what when nothing else is planned); each person can overrule with their own standing fallback, which comes first ([[D2-274 One book of standing rules per household and per person covers both…|D2-274]]'s precedence).**
