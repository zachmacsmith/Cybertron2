# One knows one's beasts by memory and noticing, not by a list (D4-56)

*live · 24 Oct*

## The rule
**One knows one's beasts by memory and noticing, not by a list:** what one last saw of them (how many, where, when), updated when one notices them or works with them, found missing when their pasture is seen empty; and **what one's living depends on is seeded as experience** (the merchants know what their ox is for)

## Where it came from
conversation ("Everyone shouldn't have their animals listed all the time, but they should almost always see things about them, and the merchants should start maybe with some knowledge of them"); proposals/beasts_known.md (retrospective)

## What it replaced
a live list of one's household's beasts and a hint to lead the ox (built and withdrawn the same day); before that, no line at all
