# Every act and every technique a person can take is registered with… (D2-350)

*live · 24 Nov*

## The rule
**Every act and every technique a person can take is registered with required descriptions: its interface (what it does, how it is said, its arguments and every option, its cost) and what it needs, shown to each person at the detail their familiarity gives; an act or technique without them is a build error. Shapes organise: every act is listed under its shape on a line of its own, never folded into another act's sentence. Interface is never left to memory; the world is.**

## Where it came from
conversation (24 Nov: "we should add new actions, and each action should require (REQUIRE!) descriptions of interface and options and be shown to the appropriate detail. If we build a type of action and don't describe it then it pretty much doesn't exist! ... The new action shape is about how things are organised, didn't I literally ask if actions are listed under organised by this rather than hiding them behind the verb?")
