# A technique's name, what it does, what it needs and its working hints… (D2-356)

*live · 24 Nov*

## The rule
**A technique's name, what it does, what it needs and its working hints are separate fields of its row, rendered together where they are shown; nothing is encoded in one string and split apart (the 'name (hint)' label is retired).**
