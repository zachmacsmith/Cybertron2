# D2-37 · Acting for others

*live · 23 Oct · spec: [[agents - modifiers|agents/modifiers]], [[economy - agreements|economy/agreements]]*

## The rule
**Acting for others:** `for: <party>` on an act (work, give, offer, accept) does it on that party's behalf: output goes into, and payment comes from, the party's container at the node, if the actor may use it; so people work and pay for households, companies and institutions
