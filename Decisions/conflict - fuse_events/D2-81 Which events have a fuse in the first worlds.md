# Which events have a fuse in the first worlds (D2-81)

*live · 23 Oct · spec: [[conflict - fuse_events|conflict/fuse_events]], [[core - round|core/round]], [[information - noticing|information/noticing]], [[world - edges|world/edges]], [[world - roads|world/roads]]*

## The rule
**Which events have a fuse in the first worlds:** a raid on a building, only sometimes (a chance that anyone detects it at all, from a watcher, dogs, noise heard by neighbours, and how long the structure holds); fire; meetings on the road; finds on the road worth taking (goods, a body, a herd once herds wander). A traveller on the road gets these calls (an exception to [[D4-10 Both kinds of distance|D4-10]]). The fuse's length sets how much the woken can do in their one call (a short fuse one act, a long one several). Arrivals at a shut gate are not a fuse event: the dawn check, then the talk step ([[D2-5 Fuse events|D2-5]])
