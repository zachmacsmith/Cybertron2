# A state shows itself as a bundle of signs, not one line:

## Text
**A state shows itself as a bundle of signs, not one line:** each state of a thing carries several possible signs, each with its own sense, chance of being noticed and conditions (some only when certain other things are present), and each observer meets each sign on its own, so different people notice different things
