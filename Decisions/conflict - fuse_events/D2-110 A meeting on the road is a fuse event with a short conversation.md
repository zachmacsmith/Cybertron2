# A meeting on the road is a fuse event with a short conversation (D2-110)

*live · 23 Oct · spec: [[conflict - fuse_events|conflict/fuse_events]], [[world - edges|world/edges]], [[world - roads|world/roads]]*

## The rule
**A meeting on the road is a fuse event with a short conversation:** a few waves of talk, giving and offers (free, as at a node), then each party's choice: go on, turn back, change where it is going or travel with the other, attack, or flee (amends [[D2-5 Fuse events|D2-5]]'s one wave, for meetings)
