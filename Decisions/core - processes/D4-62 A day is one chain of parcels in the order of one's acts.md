# A day is one chain of parcels in the order of one's acts (D4-62)

*live · 24 Oct · spec: [[core - processes|core/processes]], [[core - world_schema|core/world_schema]], [[world - parcel_graph|world/parcel_graph]]*

## The rule
**A day is one chain of parcels in the order of one's acts:** from where one slept, to each parcel worked in turn, to where one ends the day (one's bed, or the road's end if one sets off), each leg a shortest way; kept in the day's record for the noticing rule and the map
