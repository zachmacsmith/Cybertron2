# D4-108 · Work and its hunger are felt

*live · 8 Nov · spec: [[core - processes|core/processes]]*

## The rule
(proposals/economic_calibration.md, History 8 Nov): in the night lines, a ration eaten but not the work's share is felt as the work's doing ("you ate a ration's worth, but after the day's work it was not enough"); a hard day (6 units or more) leaves one ravenous, with what one ate against a lighter day's need; pushing on is felt that night; in the morning, yesterday's pushing on and walking show as "worn out from yesterday's work: N units fewer"

## Where it came from
conversation ("add the felt sign for hard work and hunger"), after E2 showed no agent reasoning about work's food cost in 296 agent-days
