# D2-69 · The scheduler's layers

*live · 23 Oct · spec: [[core - processes|core/processes]], [[environment - scheduler|environment/scheduler]]*

## The rule
**The scheduler's layers:** eras (years), years (the calendar and each year's severity), spells (days), incidents (a day at a place) and spreads, each setting the odds of the next; an incident's hazard = base x weather x season x land kind x exposure (the place's own state)
