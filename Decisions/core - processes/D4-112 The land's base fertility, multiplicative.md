# The land's base fertility, multiplicative (D4-112)

*live · 8 Nov · spec: [[core - processes|core/processes]], [[core - world_schema|core/world_schema]]*

## The rule
(proposals/base_fertility.md, retrospective): a parcel's `land.base_fertility` multiplies every level its soil comes back toward (rest, grazing, dung, a crop's restoring) and a new clearing's start, so dung cannot make poor ground rich; the capacity model follows; default 1.0 (older worlds unchanged); world schema version 4

## Where it came from
conversation (37.2's fixed/slow/fast land properties; Zach: "river plains are very fertile but even adding dung can't make desert sand fertile enough"; "yes multiplicative if that makes sense")
