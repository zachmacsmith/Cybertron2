# Renewal and packs; each stock its own rule (D4-104)

*live · 7 Nov · spec: [[core - processes|core/processes]], [[core - world_schema|core/world_schema]]*

## The rule
(proposals/process_model.md, History 7 Nov): the engine's word for how a stock comes back is **renewal** (was regime), and switchable bundles of data are **packs** (was systems; `systems:` still read). A stock's own record may name its renewal rule and its **response to density** (the take scales by (S/K) ** b; default 1), so an ecosystem decides how its stock comes back and answers being taken

## Where it came from
conversation ("Why did you call it a regime? I feel like that will get confused with societies? ... each environment / ecosystem should be able to decide its own regeneration / growth / population mechanisms and how this relates to being fished"); the names chosen under "Don't ask me anything just do it"

names in [[D4-103 The engine overhaul goes ahead, in phases|D4-103]]'s records
