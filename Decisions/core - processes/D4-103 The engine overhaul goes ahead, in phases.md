# The engine overhaul goes ahead, in phases (D4-103)

*live · 7 Nov · spec: [[core - processes|core/processes]], [[core - world_schema|core/world_schema]], [[measurement - capacity|measurement/capacity]]*

## The rule
(proposals/engine_overhaul.md): **1** the declared process model with clocks (process_model.md), behaviour unchanged and proven by replay, with fishing, salt and a fish run as its acceptance test (content: water_and_preservation.md); **2** calibration and labour (economic_calibration.md: the time scale, harvest windows, pulses, processing, work costing food with the watch, overexertion and fatigue, skill fading, distance, the fallback's limit, wear and upkeep); **3** variance and risk (the environment scheduler's economic kinds, building risks, woods feeding game, bad-year capacity); **4** a larger experiment; **5** levels of fire, water and shelter as needs (and beasts' feed, carts) after it. Small experiments run between phases, alongside building the next; a deep breakdown for Zach after phase 2 before going on

## Where it came from
conversation ("leave levels of fire, water as a need, and shelter as a need as a second phase to revisit ... the environment scheduler ... as phase 3 ... We should do a larger experiment before moving to that ... design small experiments to run between each phase ... You can do the first 2 phases and then I'll want a deep break down")
