# D4-111 · Every weather query names its place

*live · 8 Nov · spec: [[core - processes|core/processes]], [[information - signs|information/signs]]*

## The rule
(proposals/weather_scheduler.md, History): the weather, wet, snow, the cold, the senses' share and the words take a node or a road, with no region-wide default and no reference node; one acting reads where they are (Here), the night and the land read the place of each house, sleeper, fire, field, parcel and container, signs the place of what they show; a road reads the mean of its ends; the capacity model reads each field's place and weights stores, fuel and upkeep by who lives where; a call without a place is an error. With the scheduler, visibility comes from rain, snow, fog and wind values, and graded sky signs (killing frost, heat, drizzle, downpour, strong and violent wind, turned cold or mild)

conversation (Zach: "if this is going through a proper system, it should have only naturally read local weather"; "shouldn't every caller usually have a place, so the default should fall back to whatever 'here' is for them"; the go: "and do these")
