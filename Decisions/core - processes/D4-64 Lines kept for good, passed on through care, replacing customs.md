# Lines kept for good, passed on through care, replacing customs (D4-64)

*live · 1 Nov · spec: [[core - processes|core/processes]], [[information - memory|information/memory]], [[life - birth_and_death|life/birth_and_death]], [[seeding - populations|seeding/populations]]*

## The rule
**Lines kept for good, passed on through care, replacing customs:** beside the rewritten memory each person has places for lines it wants to remember for good (a sentence each, kept word for word, shown at every opening); at the end of each stretch it may keep a new line or let one go for something that matters more. Whoever cares for a child may pass on a sentence with the care; if several care that day, whoever gives the most food passes theirs on; each line passed on in childhood adds a place (up to a limit). At coming of age a youth has its childhood lines and one place more; further places open evenly over adult life by the scheduler. Customs become kept lines seeded only to those alive when a world starts (none to those born in a run); a world may fix lines so they cannot be let go; line length, places and schedule are set per run
