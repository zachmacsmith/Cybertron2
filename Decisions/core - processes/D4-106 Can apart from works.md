*live · 8 Nov · spec: [[core - processes|core/processes]], [[core - world_schema|core/world_schema]], [[information - signs|information/signs]], [[measurement - capacity|measurement/capacity]]*

## The rule
(proposals/crop_growth.md): an act is refused only when it cannot be done or one has no idea how; a crop grows by its season's rate, its yield scaled by how well it grew; seed lies and is lost in cold ground; a tender crop thins with each cold night; winter grain wants a winter; reaping before ripe gives a green crop (little food, fodder, dried to hay); a hoe or plough turns a crop under; seed on turf takes at a tenth; hay and salt by the season; crops give signs of each state, plain when bad. Replaces [[D4-105 Sowing windows are lore, not rules|D4-105]]'s silent failure out of the window
