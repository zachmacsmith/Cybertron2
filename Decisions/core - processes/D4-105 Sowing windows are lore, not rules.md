# Sowing windows are lore, not rules (D4-105)

*live · 8 Nov · spec: [[core - processes|core/processes]], [[core - world_schema|core/world_schema]]*

## The rule
(proposals/economic_calibration.md, History 8 Nov): the agents' rules do not state when crops are sown; a crop sown out of its window is not refused and does not come up (nobody is told why); households holding a field start with the sowing lore as a line kept for good (lore.py), passed on as kept lines are, so it is a farmers' advantage. Skill fades only after half a year unpractised, by half a year of what can be lost, never below 60% of one's best gain, and comes back three times faster while below one's best. Millstones wear after 2000 grain whether an ox or the river turns them; a leat silts in a year; an oven after 10 firings. With it: the walk stays on the day's route (the shortest path joining one's jobs in order, [[D4-62 A day is one chain of parcels in the order of one's acts|D4-62]]), at 0.1 of a unit a leg past the first two (was 0.25 a parcel past two: about 0.6 of a unit a person a day in E2's first 14 days, now about 0.13)

## Where it came from
conversation ("They shouldn't be told sowing windows. Surely if they sow in the wrong times they just don't grow? Sowing windows should be long term memories families start with and have to pass on ... It also becomes a knowledge advantage for farmers. 8 days unpractised for skills seems fast? They should only fade some amount and also if they were good at it in the past it should be much easier to relearn. Also, milling needs repairs after just 3 actions feels kinda off"); Zach, on the walk: "the routes shouldn't return home between work? They should be finding the shortest path that joins them in order" (they do; Wenbridge's parcels meet only at the green); values are 37.4's first values

## What it replaced
sowing refusal, the rules' crop line, phase 2's fade and wear values ([[D4-103 The engine overhaul goes ahead, in phases|D4-103]])
