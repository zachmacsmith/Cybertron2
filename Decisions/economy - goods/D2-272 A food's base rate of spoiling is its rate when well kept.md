# A food's base rate of spoiling is its rate when well kept (D2-272)

*live · 22 Nov · spec: [[economy - goods|economy/goods]]*

## The rule
**A food's base rate of spoiling is its rate when well kept (covered, dry, out of the sun), the rate its preserving gives it; stores and bins remove the outside penalties (rain, sun, heat, damp, open ground) but never slow a food below its base; better stores each remove a particular risk (a cellar's cool for the foods heat ruins, a granary's dryness and height against damp and vermin, smoke against flies, brine in a barrel), not a blanket multiplier**
