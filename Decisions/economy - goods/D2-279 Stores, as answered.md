# Stores, as answered (D2-279)

*live · 22 Nov · spec: [[economy - goods|economy/goods]]*

## The rule
**Stores, as answered: goods are never kept at the rate of a building's best fitting; each kind of store removes risks for the food in it; every good has a place it is stored in, usually set behind the standing rules but visible and changeable by the agents in more detail if they want; a store is a space and the containers in it (sacks, baskets, chests and bins, barrels later), each container made separately with its capacity, what it protects and what it suits; stores keep their real capacities (the scale gap against the compressed year revisited later); the stores of storage.md (loose floor, sacks, baskets, bins, chests, barrels, granary floor and staddle granary, cellar by footprint on dry ground, sealed pit, smoke loft, woodshed, vault) as the set; vermin for now a basic system: each part of a store loses a share a night by its exposure, cats, owls and terriers cut it, spilt or heaped food draws more, and more vermin bring signs and raise the baseline chance of sudden illness; the household's book of standing rules runs its stores ([[D2-274 One book of standing rules per household and per person covers both…|D2-274]]), the system prompt giving the grammar it needs and the ways of managing a household seeded as starting memories, chiefly for the head, to pass to the heir; the locked chest deferred**
