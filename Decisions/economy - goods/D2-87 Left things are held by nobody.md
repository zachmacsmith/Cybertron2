# Left things are held by nobody (D2-87)

*live · 23 Oct · spec: [[economy - goods|economy/goods]], [[life - birth_and_death|life/birth_and_death]], [[world - places|world/places]], [[world - things_in_places|world/things_in_places]]*

## The rule
**Left things are held by nobody:** anyone can pick them up ([[D4-23 Holding is a recognised claim|D4-23]]); by default the dead leave everything where they die. In the open, food and bodies **spoil much faster and rot**, and rotting food and bodies are things with a disease risk ([[D2-71 Risks attach to things|D2-71]]; disease itself: OPEN [[Q-16 Disease mortality, the units of the sick, immunity, nursing, a healer|Q-16]])
