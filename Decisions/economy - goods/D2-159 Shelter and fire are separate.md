# D2-159 · Shelter and fire are separate

*live · 24 Oct · spec: [[economy - goods|economy/goods]], [[economy - lodging|economy/lodging]]*

## The rule
**Shelter and fire are separate:** where one sleeps (out, bedroll, tent, a lean-to camp, a house) sets the night's food; a fire there cooks, and in the cold warms; a fire in the open burns 2 timber ([[D4-49 A fire in the open for a night takes about 2 timber|D4-49]]), a camp's 1, a house's by its hearth (0.5, 2 cold); a fire in the open or a camp's is **shared by up to 4** sleeping there; a fire ring in a house half-warms 6 spaces, a hearth fully warms 12

## Where it came from
forests.md §2a

## What it replaced
a fire per person in the open; one fire warming any number
