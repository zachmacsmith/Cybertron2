# Farming's minimum is short, the other stages optional (D2-11)

*live · 22 Oct · spec: [[economy - goods|economy/goods]], [[economy - production|economy/production]]*

## The rule
**Farming's minimum is short, the other stages optional:** sow and reap give a crop; breaking the soil, weeding, manure, rotation and grazing the fallow each cost work and give a better return (more food per plot, or soil that does not wear out), worth most to those who can do them and at scale

## Where it came from
02l §1-§2

## What it replaced
the old engine's fixed sow, tend, reap
