# Cattle are one kind with three roles:

- **state**: live
- **source**: 02o; conversation
- **replaces_text**: four kinds (cows, bulls, oxen, calves) as separate goods

## Text
**Cattle are one kind with three roles:** cows (milk, calves), oxen (trained steers that pull) and bulls (needed for breeding; whoever holds the bull controls a herd's breeding); they share feeding, herding and slaughter rules; young are not a separate kind within a run
