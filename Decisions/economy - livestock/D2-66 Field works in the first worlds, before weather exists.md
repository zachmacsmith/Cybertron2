# Field works in the first worlds, before weather exists:

- **state**: live
- **source**: proposals/farming.md; conversation

## Text
**Field works in the first worlds, before weather exists:** ditches and banks on wet ground and terraces on hill ground, built as projects on a plot (led by someone who knows the technique), part of the plot thereafter, with upkeep each season. Until 37.4's weather exists, wet ground yields x0.75 and hill ground x0.6, and the works bring each to x1.0; weather replaces these fixed effects later
