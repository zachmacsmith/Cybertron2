# D4-41 · Oxen are a role within cattle, not a kind

*live · 23 Oct · spec: [[economy - livestock|economy/livestock]]*

## The rule
**Oxen are a role within cattle, not a kind:** one kind (cattle) whose members are cows, bulls, steers, oxen (steers trained to the yoke, [[D2-43 The basic set and the ten trades|D2-43]]) and calves; the role changes by training or growing up, the kind never

## Where it came from
38.20 B3; 02o §1; conversation ("a role within cattle")

## What it replaced
replaced in part by [[D2-63 Cattle are one kind with three roles|D2-63]] (three roles: cows, oxen, bulls; young counted with the herd)
