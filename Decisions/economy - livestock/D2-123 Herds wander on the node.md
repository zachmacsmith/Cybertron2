## The rule
**Herds wander on the node:** a fed herd on good pasture 0 to 1 steps a day, a hungry or drawn one up to about 8; each head eats and tramples about 1% of a field's crop a day; from a parcel where a road leaves, about 0.1 a step of taking the road
