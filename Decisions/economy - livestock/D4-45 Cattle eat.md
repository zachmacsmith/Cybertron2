# Cattle eat (D4-45)

*live · 23 Oct · spec: [[economy - livestock|economy/livestock]]*

## The rule
**Cattle eat:** they graze the pasture they are on, and eat stored fodder where there is no grazing (winter); too many beasts on a pasture wear it down, and its grazing recovers slowly; the remedy is people's (move the herd, slaughter, keep fewer)
