# Breeding happens on its own

- **state**: live
- **source**: 38.20 B2; 02o §2; conversation ("for breeding I agree, and then they could move the bull into a different field if they don't want to breed")

## Text
**Breeding happens on its own** wherever a bull is with the cows and the herd is fed; keeping the bull apart (another pasture) stops it, so breeding is managed by where the bull is kept (with [[D2-63 Cattle are one kind with three roles|D2-63]]: whoever holds the bull controls breeding)
