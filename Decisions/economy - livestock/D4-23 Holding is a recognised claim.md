# Holding is a recognised claim (D4-23)

*live · 22 Oct · spec: [[economy - livestock|economy/livestock]], [[economy - production|economy/production]], [[economy - transport|economy/transport]], [[institutions - membership|institutions/membership]], [[world - holding|world/holding]], [[world - things_in_places|world/things_in_places]]*

## The rule
The engine enforces only possession; claims (rights, grounds, since) are records and memories; recognition by custom or an institution's register makes a holding, and an institution's code may bind its own members; individuals, households, institutions and joint holders can hold; two claims make a dispute (feud, border); land is counted in whole plots, holdings divided, plots never split; viability is land per mouth; one routed primitive moves a claim
