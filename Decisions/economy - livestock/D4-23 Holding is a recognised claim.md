# Holding is a recognised claim.

- **state**: live
- **source**: spec/world/holding.md
- **replaces_text**: the old engine's holder field enforced by the engine

## Text
**Holding is a recognised claim.** The engine enforces only possession; claims (rights, grounds, since) are records and memories; recognition by custom or an institution's register makes a holding, and an institution's code may bind its own members; individuals, households, institutions and joint holders can hold; two claims make a dispute (feud, border); land is counted in whole plots, holdings divided, plots never split; viability is land per mouth; one routed primitive moves a claim
