# Speaking in a building is one general action for any building (D4-30)

*live · 23 Oct · spec: [[information - speech|information/speech]]*

## The rule
whoever has access to a building may speak in it, and everyone with access who is at the node hears; access is data on each building (a house: its household and anyone let in; a public building: everyone at the node)

## Where it came from
conversation ("should be a general action they can do for any building")

## What it replaced
M1's speech in one's own house only
