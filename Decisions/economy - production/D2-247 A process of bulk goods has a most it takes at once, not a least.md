# A process of bulk goods has a most it takes at once, not a least (D2-247)

*live · 20 Nov · spec: [[economy - production|economy/production]]*

## The rule
**A process of bulk goods has a most it takes at once, not a least: one may process less for the same work, which is less worth it, and asking for more processes the most (the drying rack's up-to batch, brief 4c §6.1, made the rule for milling, baking, salting, drying hay, leaching, charcoal and cheese); making a whole thing (a tool, a plough, a tent) still takes its inputs whole**

## Where it came from
conversation (20 Nov: "just as hearths should have 8 as a max for hanging meat, surely the mill should have its max set, rather than a minimum? you should be able to mill less, it is just less worth it? If you try to mill any amount greater than the max, it just instead mills only the max?")
