# The fishing, hunting and materials network (D2-264)

*live · 22 Nov · spec: [[economy - production|economy/production]]*

## The rule
**The fishing, hunting and materials network (proposals/hunt_fish_materials.md), as answered: each piece of gear has its own life by what it is made of (some wear fast, some slowly); every good's weight is stated in kilograms and the carry limit read against it; the withy bed keeps its three world years to full yield; flax is grown in a close and retting it in water fouls that water; one set act lays up to about 6 traps; the water holds a shallows stock of its own, and the relative stocks of shallows, open water and runs vary with the kind of water the world generates, the signs following; fowling is split from fish nets; the snare redesign and edges are written into the design**
