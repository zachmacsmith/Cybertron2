# Smoking in three depths (D2-271)

*live · 22 Nov · spec: [[economy - production|economy/production]]*

## The rule
**Smoking in three depths: a hot smoke (hours; keeps days to a couple of weeks) at a hearth or open fire, a keeping smoke (keeps about a season) in a smoke hut, a hard cure (salted first, then a long cold smoke; keeps most of a year and travels) only in a timber or stone smokehouse; a wattle smoke hut takes hot and keeping smokes and can burn with its batch, a timber or stone smokehouse all three, holds more a space (tiers, an upper storey) and seldom burns; smoke racks are made with withies and a timber**
