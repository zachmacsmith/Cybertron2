# A forest is three stocks (D2-156)

*live · 24 Oct · spec: [[economy - production|economy/production]], [[environment - resources|environment/resources]]*

## The rule
**A forest is three stocks:** standing trees, young growth and dead wood. Trees shed dead wood in proportion to how many there are; felling takes trees and leaves brash as dead wood; young growth regrows fast, not where beasts graze; below about 40% of its trees a forest turns to scrub. Woods at roughly real magnitudes by kind (old forest about 2,500 timber a unit of size, a managed wood 600, a copse 300); each world sets the push by how small and worn its near woods are
