# Two clocks (D2-214)

*live · 16 Nov · spec: [[environment - calendar|environment/calendar]], [[environment - land|environment/land]], [[environment - wildlife|environment/wildlife]]*

## The rule
(proposals/environment_systems.md; docs/review/37_growth/02zzn_environment_prototype.md): the world runs one clock on day schedules (the weather's events: a storm's rain, a cold snap, wind; work and nights) and one on seasonal and yearly scales, set by how many days the world's year has: the seasonal stocks (soil water, groundwater and wells, snowpack, the slow layer) move about 365 ÷ the year's days real days in each engine day, so a summer draws the soil down and a winter fills it whatever the year's length

## Where it came from
conversation (16 Nov: "I think there should be two clocks, one working on day schedules and one on seasonal / yearly scales by how many days are in a year"); OPEN [[Q-52 Environment systems|Q-52]] (7)

## What it replaced
every rate real per engine day (one clock: no seasonal loops in a 24 to 40 day year)
