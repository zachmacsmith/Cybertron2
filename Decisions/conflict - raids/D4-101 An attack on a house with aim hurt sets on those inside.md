# An attack on a house with aim hurt sets on those inside (D4-101)

*live · 7 Nov · spec: [[conflict - raids|conflict/raids]]*

## The rule
**An attack on a house with aim hurt sets on those inside:** the raid's break-in, then a fight with each person inside in turn (a kill or a wound each), until one drives the attacker out; nothing is taken; aim rob stays a raid for food

## Where it came from
conversation ("for the attack on a house, if they decide hurt then make it so that they attack the people in it. Multiple of them."); proposals/house_attacks.md (retrospective)

## What it replaced
an attack on a house was a raid for food whatever its aim (OPEN [[L-64 An attack on a house ignores its aim Done|L-64]])
