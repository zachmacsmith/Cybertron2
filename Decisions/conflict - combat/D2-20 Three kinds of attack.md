# D2-20 · Three kinds of attack

*live · 22 Oct · spec: [[conflict - combat|conflict/combat]], [[conflict - defence|conflict/defence]], [[conflict - raids|conflict/raids]], [[economy - buildings|economy/buildings]], [[economy - goods|economy/goods]]*

## The rule
**Three kinds of attack:** on a person; on a building (a break-in whose work depends on the structure, then a fight with whoever defends); on a whole node (targets drawn by exposure, value and hardness, so a stone building is less likely to be picked and stronger when it is). **Walls multiply the defence of those inside**

## Where it came from
02k §3

## What it replaced
combat.py's raid on a house only
