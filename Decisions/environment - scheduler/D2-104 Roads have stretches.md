# Roads have stretches (D2-104)

*live · 23 Oct · spec: [[environment - scheduler|environment/scheduler]], [[world - edges|world/edges]], [[world - roads|world/roads]]*

## The rule
**Roads have stretches (one per day of the edge) and landmarks, kept very simple:** an edge with no landmarks behaves as one terrain throughout, as now; landmarks are optional data kinds where more complex events can emerge and connect. The edge's terrain (OPEN [[Q-17 Edge terrain data|Q-17]]) is its stretches' default

## Where it came from
proposals/road_layout.md §1

## What it replaced
one place per edge; stretches without landmarks
