# The morning message built from beliefs

- **state**: live
- **source**: conversation ("Can you try with building out this new belief prompt building system"; "day and weather feels like it should be senses"); proposals/agent_view.md points 1 and 9
- **replaces_text**: the morning message with "Today you could" and the whole place each day (prompt.turn_prompt), for worlds with the setting

## Text
**The morning message built from beliefs** (world setting `view: beliefs`): the opening of a stretch shows the whole of what one believes of here (the layout, each part with what one last saw of it and when; who is here, household and kin first, the rest counted); later mornings show only what changed since the last (people come and gone, what one saw anew that the day's notice did not already say, one's house and its store, new buildings); the day and the weather as senses ("Now"); no list of what one could do today; what one worked on, one sees (its parcel's or building's state into one's beliefs, ambient lines left out)
