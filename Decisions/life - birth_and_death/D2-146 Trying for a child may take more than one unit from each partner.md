# D2-146 · Trying for a child may take more than one unit from each partner

*live · 24 Oct · spec: [[life - birth_and_death|life/birth_and_death]]*

## The rule
**Trying for a child may take more than one unit from each partner,** with diminishing returns (about 0.20 a night for 1 unit each, 0.32 for 2, 0.39 for 3, 0.48 for 6)
