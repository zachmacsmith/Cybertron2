# Shorter waits for children, a higher chance:

- **state**: live
- **source**: conversation ("your 2, 1, 0.8 makes sense. 5 days was way too long"); proposals/fertility.md; 02ze
- **replaces_text**: [[D2-147 The nursing gap is about 4 days; pregnancy adds about 0.3 food|D2-147]]'s 5-day pregnancy and 4-day gap; [[D2-146 Trying for a child may take more than one unit from each partne|D2-146]]'s cap of 0.5 (its diminishing returns kept)

## Text
**Shorter waits for children, a higher chance:** pregnancy **2 days** and the gap after a birth **1 day** (at the default lifespan; scaled, [[D2-153 The lifespan is a world setting that scales every part of a lif|D2-153]]), and conception **0.8 x (1 - e^(-0.5 x units))** a night (about 0.31 for 1 unit each, 0.51 for 2, 0.62 for 3); as built a population could not replace itself at any effort (02ze: R0 0.59 trying every night); now about 3 births a woman trying every night, replacement at a little over half the nights. Limits should be soft (feeding, the vulnerable young) rather than hard waits (proposals/fertility.md §4, §5, open)
