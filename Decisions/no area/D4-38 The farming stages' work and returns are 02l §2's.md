# The farming stages' work and returns are 02l §2's

- **state**: live
- **source**: 38.20 A1; farming.md §1; conversation ("I agree with proposal's answer for A1")
- **replaces_text**: the engine's sow, weed and reap only

## Text
**The farming stages' work and returns are 02l §2's** (calibrate): breaking the soil by hoe (about 3 units a plot) or ox plough (about 0.75) gives a harvest about 1.5 times larger; weeding adds up to half on a saturating curve (about +24%, +37%, +43% for 1, 2, 3 units); manure about 1 unit with dung; reaping about 1.5 units a plot with a sickle, 2.5 bare-handed, threshing included; every stage but sowing and reaping stays optional ([[D2-11 Farming's minimum is short, the other stages optional|D2-11]], [[D2-55 Labour need not be scarce; land binds, and more labour grows out|D2-55]])
