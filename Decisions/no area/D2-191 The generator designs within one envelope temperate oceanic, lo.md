# The generator designs within one envelope: temperate oceanic, lowland to moor, settled

- **state**: live
- **source**: conversation ("build this out")
- **replaces_text**: one terrain mixing landform, vegetation and distance; lakes in every hollow; forest wherever loam or clay

## Text
**The generator designs within one envelope: temperate oceanic, lowland to moor, settled** (temperate_landscapes.md): seven landscape systems (river country, scarp and vale, sand country, upland, fen, coast, wildwood); ecosystems by each system's palette; hollows breached so land drains, lakes only in fens and uplands; land cleared by how long settled (`settled:` frontier 0.6, settled 0.3, long settled 0.15 of the land in wood); deep forest the core of any large wood; every configuration checked to make its own landscape; delta and rift wait outside the envelope
