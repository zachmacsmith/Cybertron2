# One knows one's beasts by memory and noticing, not by a list:

- **state**: live
- **source**: conversation ("Everyone shouldn't have their animals listed all the time, but they should almost always see things about them, and the merchants should start maybe with some knowledge of them"); proposals/beasts_known.md (retrospective)
- **replaces_text**: a live list of one's household's beasts and a hint to lead the ox (built and withdrawn the same day); before that, no line at all

## Text
**One knows one's beasts by memory and noticing, not by a list:** what one last saw of them (how many, where, when), updated when one notices them or works with them, found missing when their pasture is seen empty; and **what one's living depends on is seeded as experience** (the merchants know what their ox is for)
