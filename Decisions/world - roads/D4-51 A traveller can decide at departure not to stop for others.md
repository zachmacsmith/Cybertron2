# A traveller can decide at departure not to stop for others (D4-51)

*live · 24 Oct · spec: [[world - roads|world/roads]]*

## The rule
**A traveller can decide at departure not to stop for others:** any meeting on the road then passes on its own, with no call, described to both sides; bandits and ambushes will act differently (later)

## Where it came from
conversation ("when deciding ahead of time when they set off they can also decide if they do not want to stop and talk to others (bandits / ambushes will act differently) which means any interactions will pass on automatically with appropriate descriptions")

## What it replaced
every meeting a call for both parties ([[D2-110 A meeting on the road is a fuse event with a short conversation|D2-110]])
