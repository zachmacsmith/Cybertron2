# Good places on the way are not stopping points

- **state**: live
- **source**: road_layout.md §1
- **replaces_text**: stopping points as places to stop on the way

## Text
**Good places on the way are not stopping points** (amends [[D2-107 First-world landmarks|D2-107]]): passing a meadow, wild fruit trees or a spring is still a default trip with nothing to decide, but it changes the traveller's state on arrival (fed on the way, a little food gathered). A traveller can choose to stop at one only in advance, at departure, and only if it knows the place (has passed it before or been told)
