# D2-109 · Good places on the way are not stopping points

*live · 23 Oct · spec: [[world - roads|world/roads]]*

## The rule
(amends [[D2-107 First-world landmarks|D2-107]]): passing a meadow, wild fruit trees or a spring is still a default trip with nothing to decide, but it changes the traveller's state on arrival (fed on the way, a little food gathered). A traveller can choose to stop at one only in advance, at departure, and only if it knows the place (has passed it before or been told)

road_layout.md §1

## What it replaced
stopping points as places to stop on the way
