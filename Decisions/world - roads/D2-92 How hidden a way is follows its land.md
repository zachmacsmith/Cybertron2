# How hidden a way is follows its land (D2-92)

*live · 23 Oct · spec: [[world - roads|world/roads]]*

## The rule
**How hidden a way is follows its land:** its parcel's cover (forest, scrub, rock hide; open ground does not), how much it is used, and changes to the land (felling exposes it; regrowth where the land regrows hides it again), not a fixed flag

## Where it came from
hidden_ways.md §1-2

## What it replaced
a fixed visibility; growing back after a fixed time
