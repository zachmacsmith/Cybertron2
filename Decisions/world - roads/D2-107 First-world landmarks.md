*live · 23 Oct · spec: [[world - roads|world/roads]]*

## The rule
**First-world landmarks:** ford, bridge, pass, narrows, spring; and **stopping points** (a spring, a meadow, wild fruit trees) where travellers can gather on the way or arrive more rested, more so if they know of the place
