# D2-105 · Travel decides itself most of the time

*live · 23 Oct · spec: [[world - roads|world/roads]]*

## The rule
**Travel decides itself most of the time:** minor trouble on the road is resolved automatically, by the traveller's choices at departure ("if there is trouble, turn back", "keep pushing") or a default, and reported on arrival with what it cost; there is still a chance of a fuse call, and more extreme or more complex situations (challenges that interact, other people, a serious risk) give one, with more choices (within [[D2-80 Fuse events are rare|D2-80]])

road_layout.md §3

## What it replaced
only fixed defaults; a call for every challenge
