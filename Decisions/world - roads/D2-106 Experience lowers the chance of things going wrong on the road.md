# D2-106 · Experience lowers the chance of things going wrong on the road

*live · 23 Oct · spec: [[world - roads|world/roads]]*

## The rule
, and whether a traveller is woken follows the risk as it stands for that traveller: an experienced one is woken only when the risk is still high for them (extreme weather), which is unusual for them, so they notice and can react
