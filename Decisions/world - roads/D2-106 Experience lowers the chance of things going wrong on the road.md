# Experience lowers the chance of things going wrong on the road

- **state**: live
- **source**: road_layout.md §3
- **replaces_text**: experience as a filter that suppresses calls by itself

## Text
**Experience lowers the chance of things going wrong on the road**, and whether a traveller is woken follows the risk as it stands for that traveller: an experienced one is woken only when the risk is still high for them (extreme weather), which is unusual for them, so they notice and can react
