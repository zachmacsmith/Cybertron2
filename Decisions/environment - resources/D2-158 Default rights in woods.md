# Default rights in woods (D2-158)

*live · 24 Oct · spec: [[environment - resources|environment/resources]], [[world - holding|world/holding]]*

## The rule
**Default rights in woods:** dead wood, and young growth on common land, may be gathered by anyone; trees are their holder's (the engine enforces only possession: felling another's trees is heard and disputed)
