# Fresh fish keep twice as badly as meat in a store (D2-212)

*live · 16 Nov · spec: [[environment - wildlife|environment/wildlife]]*

## The rule
(proposals/river_and_water.md §3b.9): the store's spoilage rate for fresh fish is twice meat's (about 18% a night in a house, 9% with bins), so keeping a run depends on smoking and salt
