# D2-193 · Dangerous kinds can wound or kill the hunter

*live · 8 Nov · spec: [[environment - wildlife|environment/wildlife]]*

## The rule
**Dangerous kinds can wound or kill the hunter:** a boar hunt alone, a bear, a wolf at bay; so some hunts need a crew, hounds or gear

## Where it came from
conversation ("Yes to all")

## What it replaced
hunting with no risk to the hunter
