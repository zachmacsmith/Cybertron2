# A very skilled hunter brings in a disproportionate amount of food… (D2-255)

*live · 20 Nov · spec: [[environment - wildlife|environment/wildlife]]*

## The rule
**A very skilled hunter brings in a disproportionate amount of food, more still on longer trips out to other nodes, so hunting can keep a small group; the cost is the long learning (master after about half an adult life of steady hunting) and the work of the trips, and the limit is the game, not the hunter**

## Where it came from
conversation (20 Nov: "I think being very skilled at hunting should be able to sustain smaller, it's just very labour intensive in terms of the time spent learning etc. But a single very skilled hunter should be able to bring in a quite disproportionate amount of food, especially if they travel further and go on hunting trips to different nodes")
