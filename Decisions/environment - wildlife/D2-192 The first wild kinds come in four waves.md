# D2-192 · The first wild kinds come in four waves

*live · 8 Nov · spec: [[environment - wildlife|environment/wildlife]]*

## The rule
(proposals/wildlife.md §9): wave 1 red deer, hare, trout, eel, salmon, wildfowl, raven; wave 2 wolf, wild boar, fox, wild bees; wave 3 beaver, otter, pine marten, brown bear, falcon, swan; later the husbandry kinds and pests.
