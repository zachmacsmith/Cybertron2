# The weir's runs: the river's level, then re-measure (D2-215)

*live · 16 Nov · spec: [[environment - wildlife|environment/wildlife]]*

## The rule
(OPEN [[Q-55 The weir as built, measured|Q-55]] (1)): the runs' cues read the river's own flow level on the seasonal clock ([[D2-214 Two clocks|D2-214]]), not the day's rain alone; the yearly takes are re-measured on that before the runs are resized

## Where it came from
conversation (16 Nov: "Agree with 1,2,3", to 37.2's recommendation in [[Q-55 The weir as built, measured|Q-55]])

## What it replaced
a looser rain cue; runs three to five times larger now; a wetter era
