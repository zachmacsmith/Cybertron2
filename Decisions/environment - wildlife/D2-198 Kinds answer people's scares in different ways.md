# Kinds answer people's scares in different ways:

- **state**: live
- **source**: conversation ("not all creatures should get used to scares. Some of them should just get scared off")
- **replaces_text**: habituation for every kind

## Text
**Kinds answer people's scares in different ways:** some get used to a harmless scare (boar, woodpigeons, rooks, red deer at the ricks), some are simply scared off and come back later to be scared again (roe, hares, wildfowl, fox, wolves while the fold is kept), some leave for good if disturbed at the wrong time (a heronry in spring, a beaver family trapped), and some do not answer scares at all (rats, voles, insects). Real harm (a beast killed, a dog) works on all of them (proposals/wild_systems.md §9)
