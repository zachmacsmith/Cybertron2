# Carcasses and bodies are scavenged (D2-205)

*live · 9 Nov · spec: [[environment - wildlife|environment/wildlife]]*

## The rule
(proposals/carcass_and_decay.md): a carcass or an unburied or shallowly buried body is food for the wild kinds through the raid targets and the carrion pool, in stages sized by the beast on the day's clock; a grave dug deep draws nothing; what fed there comes back for what is near
