# D2-197 · Lead times and creature memory are set in engine days, never less…

*live · 8 Nov · spec: [[environment - wildlife|environment/wildlife]]*

## The rule
**Lead times and creature memory are set in engine days, never less than one;** stocks and rates keep the scaling principle (proposals/node_ecology.md). A sign leads its event by at least a day, and a creature's learned response builds and fades over engine days, not over real days scaled to the compressed year (proposals/wild_systems.md §8)

## Where it came from
conversation ("yeah set lead times in engine days")

## What it replaced
lead times scaled from real days
