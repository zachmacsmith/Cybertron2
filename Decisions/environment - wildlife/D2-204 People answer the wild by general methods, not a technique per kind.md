# People answer the wild by general methods, not a technique per kind (D2-204)

*live · 9 Nov · spec: [[environment - wildlife|environment/wildlife]]*

## The rule
(proposals/methods_and_encounters.md): general method rows (hunt, stalk, drive, snare, net, trap in water, den work, gather young, pest work, guard, shut) of the engine's technique shape, each method's take table generated from the kinds' traits; **skill in two layers**: the method's, and familiarity with a kind; methods known by upbringing and by what the land holds; the system text describes known methods without multipliers or lists of kinds; refusals do not list the catalogue; targets determinate through `looking for`
