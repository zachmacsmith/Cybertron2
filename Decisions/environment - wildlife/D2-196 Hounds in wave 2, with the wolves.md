## The rule
A hound is a trained beast held by a household and led along: it finds game over more ground, holds dangerous game at bay (less danger, [[D2-193 Dangerous kinds can wound or kill the hunter|D2-193]]), courses hares and deer, and guards houses and herds; it eats every day, is bred and trained, and can be killed or run off
