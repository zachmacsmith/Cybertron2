# Fresh fish keep as meat in hand, and alive in a trap or a weir until… (D2-211)

*live · 16 Nov · spec: [[environment - wildlife|environment/wildlife]]*

## The rule
(proposals/river_and_water.md §3b.9): fresh fish spoil at 0.3 a night in hand, as meat; fish alive in a set trap or a weir do not spoil until lifted (an otter or a spate may still take them)

## Where it came from
conversation (16 Nov: "or 30% could be fine"; "I agree that fish in the trap should be fine"); the check docs/review/37_growth/02zzm_fish_keeping_cal.py

## What it replaced
the proposal of 0.5 a night
