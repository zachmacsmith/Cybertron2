# The day's sightings at the hearth (D4-82)

*live · 2 Nov · spec: [[information - perception|information/perception]], [[information - speech|information/speech]]*

## The rule
**The day's sightings at the hearth:** with an evening, noticing runs twice: after the day's work, for the day (what stood out, what concerned one, what one attended to, glimpses of others, feeling watched, what one learned by watching), shown at the evening call as "What you saw today"; and after the night, for only what is new since (facts the day's pass already held are left out), shown next morning as "Since last evening"; the morning's changes leave out what the hearth showed

## Where it came from
conversation ("Yes then show the days sightings at the hearth"); proposals/speech_order.md

## What it replaced
the day's sightings shown only next morning, as "What you saw yesterday"
