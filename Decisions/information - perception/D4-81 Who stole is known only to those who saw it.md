# Who stole is known only to those who saw it:

- **state**: live
- **source**: conversation ("Are you told about thefts directly? How would they know?")
- **replaces_text**: the owners told the theft, with the thief's name if anyone else saw it, and a certain "missing from the store" line otherwise

## Text
**Who stole is known only to those who saw it:** a theft names the thief only to its witnesses (those at home, watching the house, or by the noticing rule's small chance nearby), never by chance afterwards; the house's people are not told a loss: they find it as anything is found, by the store's signs ([[D4-74 Events and their aftermath show themselves as bundles|D4-74]]: less than there was, footprints in snow or mud) and its count when they are home; others learn who did it only if a witness tells them
