# Three clocks, each system on one (D2-273)

*live · 22 Nov · spec: [[core - clocks|core/clocks]]*

## The rule
**Three clocks, each system on one: the day clock (a night; real rates) for work done in an hour, food eaten a night, walking and travel; the year clock (32 days, seasons of 8; real durations compressed about 1/11) for land, seasons, harvests, the wild's production and regrowth, the runs, how long goods keep, process times measured in real days or weeks, and the years a work takes to come into use; the life clock (a world's lifespan) for ageing, learning and practice, raising children and births. A duration of more than a few real days compresses with its clock; one of a day or two keeps its real nights as a floor. All food runs on the year clock, so food sources and keeping are calibrated against each other**
