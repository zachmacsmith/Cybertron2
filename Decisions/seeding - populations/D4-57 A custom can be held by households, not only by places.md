# D4-57 · A custom can be held by households, not only by places

*live · 24 Oct · spec: [[seeding - populations|seeding/populations]]*

## The rule
**A custom can be held by households, not only by places:** a world file may name the households whose members (and those born into them) hold a custom, so one house can live by exploring, another by growing its family, another by improving its land

## Where it came from
conversation ("design a similar run ... with a few households, and one having custom to explore and expand out, and another to have all the children expansion etc. ..., and one to just improve and build out the farms"); proposals/household_customs.md (retrospective)

## What it replaced
customs held by everyone at a node (OPEN [[L-25 Beliefs, values and customs|L-25]]: customs and values held by people, the full design, later) (replaced by [[D4-64 Lines kept for good, passed on through care, replacing customs|D4-64]]: customs are seeded kept lines)
