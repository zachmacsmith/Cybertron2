# Fuel in heat (D2-157)

*live · 24 Oct · spec: [[agents - actions|agents/actions]], [[economy - goods|economy/goods]], [[economy - lodging|economy/lodging]], [[economy - production|economy/production]]*

## The rule
**Fuel in heat:** a hearth needs 1 heat a normal night and 4 a cold one; timber gives 2 (felled with a tool; builds and burns), **firewood** 0.5 (weight 1; gathered by hand from dead wood, children may, or cut from young growth with a tool; burns only), charcoal 6 (and burns in a hearth)
