# Too heavy to set off: the traveller is told and chooses what to leave

- **state**: live
- **source**: conversation ("The prompt should state how much they can carry ... they can choose something to leave at a house or with a person and that house the next round finds the thing"); 38.14 §10
- **replaces_text**: M1's bare refusal "leave something behind"

## Text
**Too heavy to set off: the traveller is told and chooses what to leave**: the prompt states each good's weight and what the agent carries against what it can carry; a traveller over the limit is asked again, and may give goods to someone at the node or leave them at a house there (`leave`; that household finds them next morning: "X left 2 timber at Fenn House"), then set off, or stay
