# D4-96 · Using the store at home

*live · 2 Nov · spec: [[agents - actions|agents/actions]], [[core - world_schema|core/world_schema]]*

## The rule
**Using the store at home:** giving to a person or leaving at a place also draws on the stores here one may take from, as making, building and paying already did; every act says what it took from which store

## Where it came from
conversation ("if the thing they want to use is in their store and they are at home, can they not just automatically take from their store ... It should tell them")

## What it replaced
give from hands only; draws from stores unspoken
