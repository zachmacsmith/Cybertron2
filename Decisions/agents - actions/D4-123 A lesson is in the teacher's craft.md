# A lesson is in the teacher's craft (D4-123)

*live · 19 Nov · spec: [[agents - actions|agents/actions]]*

## The rule
**A lesson is in the teacher's craft:** a teach and a learn pair by teacher, learner and place; if the crafts named differ, the lesson is in the teacher's and both are told ("you wanted to learn A, but B taught C instead, so you learnt C"); an unanswered learn names its craft; physics `teach.any_craft_from_day` (decided: 0) keeps a resumed run's earlier days as they were (proposals/lesson_in_teachers_craft.md)

## Where it came from
conversation ("if there was a mismatch they should just end up getting a lesson on whatever the teacher is teaching")

## What it replaced
the crafts named had to match exactly, and a mismatch was reported to each as the other not setting time aside
