# D4-37 · Whoever is owed may count a debt paid

*live · 23 Oct · spec: [[agents - actions|agents/actions]], [[economy - agreements|economy/agreements]]*

## The rule
the person owed (or a member of the household owed) can release what is owing on an agreement when they judge it paid, however it was paid (to their household, in kind) or to forgive it; goods given or left by the debtor to the creditor are also credited automatically

## Where it came from
conversation ("the people that owed just be able to remove debt if they feel that they were paid off, so if they paid it to their household"); 38.17

## What it replaced
agreements settled only by the engine's settlement
