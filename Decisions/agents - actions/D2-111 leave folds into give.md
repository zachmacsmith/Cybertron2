# `leave` folds into `give` (D2-111)

*live · 23 Oct · spec: [[agents - actions|agents/actions]], [[agents - modifiers|agents/modifiers]], [[world - things_in_places|world/things_in_places]]*

## The rule
**`leave` folds into `give`:** give to a person, a household, a building or a place; leaving and hiding are giving to a place (hiding with the `hidden` modifier); a give that cannot reach its recipient (someone not here, a building one may not use) is left at its door, in the open

## Where it came from
verb_modifiers.md §5

## What it replaced
`leave` as its own verb
