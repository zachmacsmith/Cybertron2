# Too heavy to set off (D4-32)

*live · 23 Oct · spec: [[agents - actions|agents/actions]], [[economy - transport|economy/transport]], [[information - prompts|information/prompts]], [[world - edges|world/edges]], [[world - things_in_places|world/things_in_places]]*

## The rule
the prompt states each good's weight and what the agent carries against what it can carry; a traveller over the limit is asked again, and may give goods to someone at the node or leave them at a house there (`leave`; that household finds them next morning: "X left 2 timber at Fenn House"), then set off, or stay

## Where it came from
conversation ("The prompt should state how much they can carry ... they can choose something to leave at a house or with a person and that house the next round finds the thing"); 38.14 §10

## What it replaced
M1's bare refusal "leave something behind"
