# What a refusal says (D4-95)

*live · 2 Nov · spec: [[agents - actions|agents/actions]]*

## The rule
**What a refusal says:** where a good is, who took it today, how a good is made, who watched or milked the beasts, where here a craft is done, who sets a door, why work or an exchange for an absent party fails; "picked bare" and "not ripe" stay bare of hints, but a refused act at a place counts as being there for noticing; `pin` by a note's text; no naming of one known dead as head or heir

## Where it came from
conversation (Zach on the refusal audit 38.50: "the place is picked bare is valid ... they can figure that out"; "Don't tell them when it will be ripe ... failures should still see senses about the place"; "X is already being used by Y"; "say who already did it today"; "you can do it at: X, Y, Z"; "Pinning a note by text should be possible"; "Dead members cannot be head")

## What it replaced
bare refusals with no next step
