# D2-96 · Rot and burial

*live · 23 Oct · spec: [[agents - actions|agents/actions]], [[economy - goods|economy/goods]], [[world - things_in_places|world/things_in_places]]*

## The rule
**Rot and burial:** a rotting body or rotting food lies at a spot and, once disease exists (OPEN [[Q-16 Disease mortality, the units of the sick, immunity, nursing, a healer|Q-16]]), carries a risk that can reach water, food and people acting nearby; a death in a settlement is highly salient; **burying is work on the body**, an option shown only where there is a body, its labour by the ground and the cold (frozen ground is harder)
