# A beast that dies leaves a carcass (D4-52)

*live · 24 Oct · spec: [[world - things_in_places|world/things_in_places]]*

## The rule
where it fell, a dead beast as a person leaves a body: fit to eat for about a day, then it goes off. **On a road a dying beast may leave no body** (it wandered off to die) **or a hidden one** (off the road, out of sight)

## Where it came from
conversation ("Deade cattle should only be edible for like 1 day then go off (realistic no?). There should be a chance that the cattle don't leave a body, or a hidden body, when dying on the roads, since they might have wandered off etc."), answering 37.4's question whether dead beasts leave bodies; proposals/carcasses.md (retrospective)

## What it replaced
a beast that died vanished
