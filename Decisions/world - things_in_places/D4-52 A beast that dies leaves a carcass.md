# A beast that dies leaves a carcass

- **state**: live
- **source**: conversation ("Deade cattle should only be edible for like 1 day then go off (realistic no?). There should be a chance that the cattle don't leave a body, or a hidden body, when dying on the roads, since they might have wandered off etc."), answering 37.4's question whether dead beasts leave bodies; proposals/carcasses.md (retrospective)
- **replaces_text**: a beast that died vanished

## Text
**A beast that dies leaves a carcass** where it fell, a dead beast as a person leaves a body: fit to eat for about a day, then it goes off. **On a road a dying beast may leave no body** (it wandered off to die) **or a hidden one** (off the road, out of sight)
