# D2-166 · Large capital costs village-scale work

*live · 3 Nov · spec: [[economy - buildings|economy/buildings]]*

## The rule
**Large capital costs village-scale work:** a hall, a water wheel and stone building cost several times what a household can spare in a year (about 300 to 500 units for a water mill in all, about 300 for a hall: numbers to calibrate), so a village, a lord or an investor builds them; small buildings and fittings keep their costs (02zf)

## Where it came from
conversation ("1 and 2 yes"); 02zf §1

## What it replaced
the engine's hall (60 units) and water wheel (30)
