# D2-168 · Wear varies by structure and repair by size

*live · 3 Nov · spec: [[economy - buildings|economy/buildings]]*

## The rule
**Wear varies by structure and repair by size:** stone wears slowest, wattle fastest (rates to calibrate); repair takes materials and work in proportion to the building's size

## Where it came from
conversation ("this should vary"); 02zf §3

## What it replaced
one break rate and a 2-timber repair for every building
