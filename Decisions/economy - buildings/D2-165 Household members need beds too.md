# D2-165 · Household members need beds too

*live · 3 Nov · spec: [[economy - buildings|economy/buildings]]*

## The rule
**Household members need beds too:** everyone, members as well as guests, sleeps housed only in a bed (one a bed); seeded houses start with beds for their household. Proposed with it, to confirm: an infant shares its carer's bed, two children share one, and without a bed one sleeps on the floor as in a damaged house (buildings_integration.md §2b)

## Where it came from
conversation ("yes, household members need beds...ofc? The house should just start with beds")

## What it replaced
night.py's rule that members sleep at home whatever the beds
