# D2-170 · A line costs its own engraving

*live · 3 Nov · spec: [[economy - buildings|economy/buildings]]*

## The rule
**A line costs its own engraving:** no line comes with a building; each is painted or carved as work of its own (painting 2 units; carving 4 units with a tool; on stone 6, a mason's; changing or erasing another's the same plus 1: to calibrate); the description is written with the build ([[D2-169 Buildings carry what their people write|D2-169]])

## Where it came from
conversation ("adding a line should require more than just building it. An extra cost to engrave it")

## What it replaced
building_lines.md's first costs (1, 2, 3 units)
