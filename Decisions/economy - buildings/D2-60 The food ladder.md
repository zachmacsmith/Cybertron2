# The food ladder (D2-60)

*live · 23 Oct · spec: [[economy - buildings|economy/buildings]], [[economy - goods|economy/goods]], [[economy - production|economy/production]]*

## The rule
**The food ladder:** grain eaten whole (boiled at a hearth) is worth 0.7 food a grain, ground grain 1.0, bread 1.3; bread keeps (about 5% a day) and is eaten without a hearth; a stone oven bakes in batches. Farms can always eat without a mill
