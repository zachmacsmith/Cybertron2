# Space inside a parcel is a count, not positions:

- **state**: live
- **source**: conversation ("yes counts")

## Text
**Space inside a parcel is a count, not positions:** a parcel's ground for buildings (and a field's plots) is a number taken and freed, shown to agents as used and free ("the Green: 5 houses, room for 2 more"); things lying there have no position, only how plain or hidden they lie ([[D4-70 Taking from a place one does not know holds the thing is a searc|D4-70]])
