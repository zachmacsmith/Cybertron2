# Buildings carry what their people write (D2-169)

*live · 3 Nov · spec: [[economy - buildings|economy/buildings]]*

## The rule
**Buildings carry what their people write:** every building has a description and lines written on it by people (a dedication, a rule, the names of the dead); anyone who enters or attends to it can read them; others can change or deface them, at a cost in work and seen as any use of what others hold; lengths, costs, a monument and fine work proposed (proposals/building_lines.md)

## Where it came from
conversation ("every building, anyone can read, others can deface, write it up")

## What it replaced
a church as a designed building
