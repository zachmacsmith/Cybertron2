## Text
**The land in three speeds** (proposals/environment_systems.md §2): fixed (generations: elevation, exposure, position, slope, soil texture as water held and drainage, depth, base fertility, rain factor and normal rain, flow direction, river class, sea distance, height above the river, water type, springs, falls, fords), slow (the year clock, stepped at the season's turn: fertility, the wetness offset, vegetation, soil depth, channel silt, and conditions last), fast (the day clock, one stock and one rule each: soil water, snow, river level, fuel dryness, standing water, ice, loose soil, cleanness); each quantity on the place that holds it, with its clock
