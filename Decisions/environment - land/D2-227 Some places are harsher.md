# D2-227 · Some places are harsher

*live · 17 Nov · spec: [[environment - land|environment/land]]*

## The rule
(02zzn §3.3): frozen waterways and hard winters come from a place's own climate (a colder world, the uplands, the north), not from the cold era, which shifts a year by about 1 °C
