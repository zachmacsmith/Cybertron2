# One fish stock per water body

- **state**: live
- **source**: conversation (17 Nov: "The stock should not just be for the parcel, it should be for the lake?"; to 37.2's design (OPEN [[Q-59 (Closed 17 Nov D2-229.)|Q-59]]): "yes")
- **replaces_text**: a stock on each water parcel, sized by the lake within the village's own land ([[D4-120 A lake's fish by its size|D4-120]] as built)

## Text
**One fish stock per water body**: a lake's resident fish are one stock, sized by the whole lake and fished from every water parcel on it, and the wild layer's fish in its areas are the same stock; a river's resident fish are one stock per reach ([[D2-222 The environment's places|D2-222]]); the generator writes the water bodies (lakes and reaches: id, area, the areas and parcels on each) and tags each water parcel with its body; the engine holds the stock on the body; a very large lake may later be split into basins
