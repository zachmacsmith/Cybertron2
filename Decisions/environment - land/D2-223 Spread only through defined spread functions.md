- **state**: live

## Text
**Spread only through defined spread functions** (proposals/environment_systems.md §4): nothing averages across neighbours; a lateral effect travels only along a named channel (down: water, silt, fouling, ice; adjacency: fire, colonisation, pests; links: beasts, disease), each by its own defined spread function, one step a day
