# Spread only through defined spread functions (D2-223)

*live · 17 Nov · spec: [[environment - land|environment/land]]*

## The rule
(proposals/environment_systems.md §4): nothing averages across neighbours; a lateral effect travels only along a named channel (down: water, silt, fouling, ice; adjacency: fire, colonisation, pests; links: beasts, disease), each by its own defined spread function, one step a day

## Where it came from
conversation (17 Nov, to OPEN [[Q-52 Environment systems|Q-52]]: "3. yes. They should spread through defined spread functions")

## What it replaced
averaging or diffusion across parcels
