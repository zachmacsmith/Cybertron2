# The environment's places (D2-222)

*live · 17 Nov · spec: [[environment - land|environment/land]]*

## The rule
(proposals/environment_systems.md §1): the 150 m cells are the fixed land's source only and are not stepped; parcels (worked land) and areas (home, ring, wild) hold the slow and fast stocks; a river's reaches are its river-bearing areas strung on `down`; nodes hold the weather and snow. The engine-wide grid ([[Q-50 One grid or several|Q-50]]) stays open
