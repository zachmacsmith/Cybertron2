# D2-228 · Calibrate across a distribution of worlds

*live · 17 Nov · spec: [[environment - land|environment/land]]*

## The rule
weather, the environmental systems and the ecosystems are calibrated on a set of core test worlds covering a diverse range of environments (mountains, coasts, floodplains, dry and wet, cold and mild, and others), not on the few worlds of the moment; the worlds are expected to differ (their weather, their ecosystems); the check is that a calibration generalises in the expected ways and breaks nothing in the extremes, never that the worlds come out alike

## Where it came from
conversation (17 Nov: "when calibrating weather, environmental systems and ecosystem, you should have a distribution of different core test worlds covering a diverse range of potential environments not to remove diversity but see that our calibrations generalize in relatively expected ways ... Don't expect them to be the same")

## What it replaced
calibration on the worlds of each brief
