# A craft a person knows at capable or known shows, where they look (D2-263)

*live · 22 Nov · spec: core*

## The rule
**A craft a person knows at capable or known shows, where they look (their crafts in the system prompt, the make verb's line), its inputs (with its batch, "up to 64 grain"), what it gives, its units and its place or fitting; a rough idea shows its inputs as its card does; awareness shows the name only; basic crafts with no inputs show none**
