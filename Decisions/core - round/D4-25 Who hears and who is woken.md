# Who hears and who is woken

- **state**: live
- **source**: conversation ("They should only be woken up if someone says something to them directly"; "Building is just everyone with access to it... as long as they are on the node that round. Not at talk time"); 38.14 §3.1
- **replaces_text**: M1's talk step, which asked again everyone who heard anything

## Text
**Who hears and who is woken** (refines [[D2-7 Speech at a node|D2-7]] and [[D4-15 Speech is not a round late|D4-15]]): a message to one person reaches them before work and **wakes** them (they are asked again and may answer or change their plan); speech **in a building** reaches **everyone with access to it who is at the node that day** (a house: its household), wherever they are at the time; speech **to the town** reaches everyone at the node; neither wakes anyone. Answers go back the same way, in a few waves (cybertron 1's DM step)
