# Setting off in the morning or at nightfall (D4-67)

*live · 2 Nov · spec: [[core - round|core/round]], [[information - prompts|information/prompts]], [[world - roads|world/roads]]*

## The rule
**Setting off in the morning or at nightfall:** in the morning, as the day's first act, one walks the day (no work) and comes in by nightfall of the road's last day, with a night out for each day between, and spends that evening at the place one reaches; at nightfall (the default), one works the day, sleeps out, and comes in at midday with half that day's units. With an evening only

## Where it came from
conversation ("should people be able to set off either in the morning or evening ... leaving in the morning means they arrive by nightfall?"; "build all five"); proposals/departure_times.md

## What it replaced
[[D4-8 Arrive, then decide|D4-8]]'s single departure, for worlds with an evening (a one-day road cost no day's work)
