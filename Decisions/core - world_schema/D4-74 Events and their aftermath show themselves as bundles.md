# Events and their aftermath show themselves as bundles (D4-74)

*live · 2 Nov · spec: [[core - world_schema|core/world_schema]], [[information - perception|information/perception]], [[information - signs|information/signs]]*

## The rule
**Events and their aftermath show themselves as bundles:** the 18 events and states of review 38.43 (a fight or a raid in the night, a hunting kill, a house fire, a birth, food rotting in a store, a guard posted, beasts driven, things made, sleeping out, ploughing and reaping, quarrying, a theft, calving, a house breaking or mended, a burial, milking and minding) each record what their signs need and show, where they fit, four kinds of sense: to the doer as they do it (felt, only what the result does not say), to those nearby as it happens, lingering traces on later days, and signs under conditions (snow, rain, frost, the dark, dogs kept, who is inside). Old age and illness are left out (no state to show) and a threat keeps its event line
