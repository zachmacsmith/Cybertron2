# A partner's death is not shown by the partner line till it is known (D4-88)

*live · 2 Nov · spec: [[core - world_schema|core/world_schema]], [[life - birth_and_death|life/birth_and_death]]*

## The rule
**A partner's death is not shown by the partner line till it is known:** the survivor is free to partner again at once, and "Your partner: X" stays till the survivor's own belief has X dead

## Where it came from
conversation (the two fixes approved after the frontier run)

## What it replaced
the line cleared at the death, telling the survivor of it
