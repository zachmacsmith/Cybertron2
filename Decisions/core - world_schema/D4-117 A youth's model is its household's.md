# A youth's model is its household's (D4-117)

*live · 13 Nov · spec: [[core - world_schema|core/world_schema]], [[life - birth_and_death|life/birth_and_death]]*

## The rule
(proposals/peoples_benchmark.md, retrospective): in a world that gives its households models (`household_models`), a child coming of age takes its household's model; a household founded in the run takes its founder's model (`institutions.model`, world schema 10)

## Where it came from
conversation (Zach: "IF an assigned household has a given model, then new [people] should become that model when they come of age"); the founded household's rule is the default taken (OPEN)

## What it replaced
[[D4-20 One agent, one model, for life|D4-20]]'s youth model, in such worlds
