# D2-163 · Land is held in plot shares within a parcel

*live · 3 Nov · spec: [[core - world_schema|core/world_schema]], [[economy - production|economy/production]], [[world - holding|world/holding]]*

## The rule
**Land is held in plot shares within a parcel:** a parcel is the place (one cover, one state, one point on the graph); what is held, divided among heirs and disputed is a number of its plots, as world/holding.md §Plots already says (the engine as built claims whole parcels: to reconcile when Zach asks for it to be built)

## Where it came from
conversation ("yes plot shares"); space discussion of 3 Nov

## What it replaced
the engine's whole-parcel claims
