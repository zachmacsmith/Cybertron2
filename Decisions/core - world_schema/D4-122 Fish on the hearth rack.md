# D4-122 · Fish on the hearth rack

*live · 17 Nov · spec: [[core - world_schema|core/world_schema]]*

## The rule
**Fish on the hearth rack:** fish are hung to dry at a hearth or a fire ring as meat is (`dry fish`: a batch up to 8 fish, 3 nights, to `dried fish` at half the weight, food 2.0, keeping as dried meat; calibrate), sharing the rack with meat under the one-batch rule; the smokehouse unchanged (proposals/fish_on_the_rack.md)

## Where it came from
conversation ("fish can go on the hearth rack")

## What it replaced
`dry meat` took meat only; fish kept only salted or smoked
