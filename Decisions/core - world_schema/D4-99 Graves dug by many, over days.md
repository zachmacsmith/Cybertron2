# Graves dug by many, over days (D4-99)

*live · 4 Nov · spec: [[core - world_schema|core/world_schema]], [[world - things_in_places|world/things_in_places]]*

## The rule
**Graves dug by many, over days; a cairn after burial; signs of an unfinished grave:** each bury act puts in up to 2 units, kept on the body as a share of the whole grave (soft 2, stony 4, frozen 6, reckoned on the ground that day), adding up across diggers and days; a cairn (3 stone, 2 units) can be raised over a grave after the burial; an unfinished grave is seen by how far it is dug, and as frozen again, holding snow or water, or left untouched

## Where it came from
conversation ("do the burial fix. Do both: multiple people can contribute and it can be done over days. Add some senses for looking at an unfinished grave as well"; "they should be able to add a cairn to the grave after burial"); proposals/graves.md (retrospective)

## What it replaced
a burial done by one person in one act, of the whole grave's units ([[D2-96 Rot and burial|D2-96]] as built)
