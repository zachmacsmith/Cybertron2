# A slaughter shows itself (D4-71)

*live · 2 Nov · spec: [[core - world_schema|core/world_schema]], [[information - signs|information/signs]]*

## The rule
**A slaughter shows itself:** a slaughter records the day's kills on its parcel (which beast, of which herd, by whom), and shows that day and the next as a bundle of signs: the kill and the butchering seen by those at the parcel, the smell of blood and guts there, the bellow heard by those at, crossing or next to it and, less often, by anyone at the place; the doer's own felt line; the herd uneasy, the dogs wild, crows over the leavings; blood on the ground or in the snow the next day; the one who did it seen bloodied. Signs read at a spot may carry a reach (near: at the parcel today; around: also crossing or next to it; the node), and may be kept from the doer or given only to the doer. Its chances replaced in part by [[D4-75 Every perceived line about the world comes from the one noticing rule|D4-75]]: each sign is an own read by the one noticing rule, not a fixed chance by reach
