# D4-68 · Short tags, and names given

*live · 2 Nov · spec: [[core - world_schema|core/world_schema]], [[information - prompts|information/prompts]]*

## The rule
**Short tags, and names given:** groups of beasts (H1, H2, ...) and things lying at a spot (T1, T2, ...) have a tag, given when they come to be, shown in brackets wherever they are named, the same for everyone; the verbs take a tag wherever they take a name (by a herd's name, the group that has beasts in it). Anyone may name a tagged thing (`name {thing, as}`); the name is known to the namer, everyone at the place and the namer's household, who read "(H4: Old Brock)" and may use the name. People, places, parcels and buildings keep their own names; goods are quantities. Individual beasts not now. A world setting (`tags: true`)
