# One unit of space at every level (D2-154)

*live · 24 Oct · spec: [[core - world_schema|core/world_schema]], [[economy - buildings|economy/buildings]], [[world - places|world/places]]*

## The rule
**One unit of space at every level:** a parcel has ground for buildings (built ground 24 spaces a unit of size, scrub 16), a building's ground-floor rooms take it, a room has floor (small 6, medium 12, hall 30), every fitting takes floor (a bed 1; bins, hand mill, hearth, fire ring 1; oven, anvil 2; granary floor, millstones 3); people housed are the beds; adjoining rooms a quarter cheaper; upper storeys 1.5 times the cost, in stone

## Where it came from
conversation; spec/economy/buildings.md

## What it replaced
fittings without space; capacity fixed by blueprint
