# D4-60 · New ground can be cleared for fields

*live · 24 Oct · spec: [[core - world_schema|core/world_schema]], [[world - holding|world/holding]]*

## The rule
**New ground can be cleared for fields:** a meadow or pasture (6 units of work a unit of size) or scrub (10) becomes a fallow field, a wood once felled to scrub; whoever clears land nobody holds holds it

## Where it came from
conversation ("Can they not clear land at all and make new plots? Why does it matter if there is rich land if not plots?"); proposals/clearing.md (retrospective)

## What it replaced
