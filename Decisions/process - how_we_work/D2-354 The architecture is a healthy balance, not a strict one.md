# The architecture is a healthy balance, not a strict one (D2-354)

*live · 24 Nov · spec: [[process - how_we_work|process/how_we_work]]*

## The rule
**The architecture is a healthy balance, not a strict one: most of the world is data acting on general, shared primitives that are extended when a new feature needs it; a system may own its specifics where they truly belong to it; the aim is that the engine can change quickly without falling into a tangle of ad hoc fixes or becoming so rigid that ad hoc fixes are the only fast way to build. Refines [[D2-353 Everything new in the engine is built within the agreed architecture…|D2-353]].**

## Where it came from
conversation (24 Nov: "I don't want to generalise into an architecture that is too strict, but instead one that supports the healthy balance of mechanisms being owned by a system or the specifics being there, but mostly being databased and acting on generalised, shared, extended primitives ... I don't want them to fall instantly into a tangle mess or slop pile or be too rigid that the only way to keep building quickly is turn back to ad hoc fixes")
