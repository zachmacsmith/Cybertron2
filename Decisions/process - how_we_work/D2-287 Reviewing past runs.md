# Reviewing past runs (D2-287)

*live · 22 Nov · spec: [[process - how_we_work|process/how_we_work]]*

## The rule
**Reviewing past runs: every finding is checked against the fixes made since that run (the commits and decisions after its date, and the world's settings, e.g. view: beliefs) before it is taken as evidence of a current fault; a fault already fixed is reported as fixed, with the fix.**

## Where it came from
conversation (22 Nov: "make sure when reviewing past runs not accidentally taking things as evidence for past bugs of broken mechanics that have since been fixed.")
