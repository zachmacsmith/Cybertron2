# D2-101 · Smell later, and simple

*live · 23 Oct · spec: [[environment - visibility|environment/visibility]]*

## The rule
**Smell later, and simple:** smell signs carry their own salience and a short reach (the spot, the parcel, perhaps the next), not multiplied by visibility; no wind direction for now; some are very salient (the stench of a corpse, a midden, livestock)
