# Loud things mask quieter ones (D2-100)

*live · 23 Oct · spec: [[environment - visibility|environment/visibility]]*

## The rule
**Loud things mask quieter ones:** a waterfall, a horn, a fight, a mill or smithy at work covers quieter sounds near it, and itself carries further
