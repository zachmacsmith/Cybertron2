~~A grave cannot be dug in winter~~ **Done** ([[D4-99 Graves dug by many, over days; a cairn after burial; signs of an|D4-99]], 4 Nov; proposals/graves.md). **A grave cannot be dug in winter.** Burial is one person's work in one day ([[D2-96 Rot and burial|D2-96]]; data `bury`: soft 2, stony 4, frozen 6 units), and a winter day gives 5, so on a cold day in winter nobody can bury anyone; it cannot be shared or carried over. In r1 about fifteen people pledged to bury Sabina for five days; in wb2 the starving party at the Holm stayed to dig Margery's grave and died. Keep the 6, but let several people's work add up (a crew) or let a grave carry over like a begun building
