# A household as a shared book of standing rules people subscribe to (L-151)

*open · 37.2*

## Later
Zach, 22 Nov: we will definitely revisit later when we generalise households even more, because really this is just a cultural thing of chores; later create new sets of standing rules within a household and people choose which one they subscribe to; what really is a household but just a shared agreement / sense of unity? These shared practices could be a core part of what it means to be in a household (and later institutions that formalise more into constitutions), where people could alter or split off into their own. Also open: whether a cap on rules is wanted. Waits on: the standing rules build.
