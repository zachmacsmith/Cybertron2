# Beliefs, values and customs

- **state**: open

## Text
**Beliefs, values and customs** (a large future integration): customs and values held by people, not villages; seeded per village and household; passed on in childhood and by exposure (hearths, campfires, travellers); distinct from know-how (explain is about ability, this about belief); children's values permeable, adults' firmer but open by choice or repeated exposure; family values passing down, cultural evolution, a state raising children to believe something
