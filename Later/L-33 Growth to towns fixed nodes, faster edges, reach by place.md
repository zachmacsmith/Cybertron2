# Growth to towns: fixed nodes, faster edges, reach by place.

- **state**: open
- **owner**: 37.2 with 37.4
- **waits_on_text**: transport shorter than a day becomes relevant

## Text
**Growth to towns: fixed nodes, faster edges, reach by place.** Nodes stay fixed pieces of geography (never re-drawn: claims, memories, seats and the record are built on their names); a village becomes a city by filling its node (built ground, rooms, storeys) and building over its neighbours, while roads and transport shorten edges below a day, so the day's reach is computed from where one lives, the edges and one's means of travel and can span several nodes (plan ahead only at places one knows; a new place ends the plan with a call). Needs: co-presence, speech and event reach by place and graph steps rather than the node; edges that take less than a day; rooms as places; strangers. A refactor of the engine's "same node" rules (talk, offers and payment, the evening, hosting, trying, incidents, herds); meanwhile new rules are best written in terms of place and reach
