(1) **Engine-kept snapshots shown as memory:** "Lying about, that you know of" (things with their state as last seen), "As you last saw them" (people and buildings), the household's beasts as last known, home as left, prices as last heard; in the wild region run the word "empty" on set snares passed into the agents' own memories as fact while the snares held hares (02zze); memory.md already says the engine keeps no list of marked events ([[D4-61 An agent rewrites its one memory each stretch, within a length t|D4-61]]). (2) **Agreements:** every open agreement of one's own and one's household's shown each turn with terms, dues and arrears, including ones made by others in the household (agreements.md "What agents see"), and goods legs settled automatically when both are at one node ([[D2-32 The settlement rule|D2-32]]); 38.68 finds 121 people keeping their own debt notes anyway, little goods credit, and relational credit only where the engine leaves a gap (work legs). (3) **Social structure the engine imposes** rather than people making it: automatic settlement, household-wide knowledge of agreements, pooled household stores, witnesses recorded but never perceived. Proposed direction (37.2, 12 Nov, not decided): the engine keeps what each person knows of (perception.md) but shows only what they wrote; agreements as events both saw, paid by deliberate acts, with written tallies or bonds ([[D2-24 The first-world goods include copper, salt, documents, hides and|D2-24]]) and institutions' ledgers ([[D2-33 Ledgers|D2-33]]) as the reliable record; deals and defaults perceived by witnesses; each change first as a world option against the null arm.
