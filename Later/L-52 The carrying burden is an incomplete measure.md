# The carrying burden is an incomplete measure

- **state**: open
- **owner**: 37.2
- **waits_on_text**: the ladder's results; transport ([[L-53 Transport and traders, built out|L-53]])

## Text
**The carrying burden is an incomplete measure** (economy.py; 02zl §7). It assumes the short village carries everything itself, on foot, with empty return trips; it counts any ox as one ox, with no harness check, and ignores handcarts. Better: the share of the region's labour spent on transport, whoever carries, with two-way loads, reported on foot, with what the world holds, and with an ox cart or a trader. A warning only ([[D2-190 Dependence is warned of, never refused|D2-190]])
