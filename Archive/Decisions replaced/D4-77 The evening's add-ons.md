# The evening's add-ons (D4-77)

*replaced · 2 Nov · spec: [[information - speech|information/speech]]*

## The rule
(
2. replaced by [[D4-80 The memory is asked in its own call after the night, not at the…|D4-80]]):
1. a meeting may be called in the evening (`meet {at, about}`) for the next morning; everyone at the place finds it in their morning message; those whose plan has `come` (a unit) meet before work for up to three rounds, hear all that is said, may give their plan again, and witness what is offered there (kept as a lasting memory)
2. on the last day of a stretch the memory request is put to each person at their evening call, not in a call of its own
3. a world may have the plan made at the hearth (`evening: plan`): the evening reply's acts beyond the night's are tomorrow's plan, and at dawn one is asked again only if something new reached one (a sign at dawn, something that concerned one, a birth, death or missed meal in one's household, a meeting called)
