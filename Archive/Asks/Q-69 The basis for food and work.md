# The basis for food and work (Q-69)

*closed, by zach · Zach (37.2, 37.4)*

## The question
from proposals/shared_units.md (37.4, 19 Nov), its open questions 1 to 3
1. one food is a day's need of how many kcal (2,000? 2,400? a working adult's 2,500 to 3,000?), and are children's and elders' needs fractions of it?
2. how many hours is a unit of work (the day now has 5 to 7 units)?
3. are the edible-share and availability factors shown to people, e.g. in a craft's description at higher familiarity ([[D2-251 Every technique, fitting and thing to build says what it does and why…|D2-251]])?

**Recommended:** (1) one food = 2,500 kcal, a working adult's day; children 0.6, elders 0.8; (2) a unit about 2 hours, a working day 5 to 6 units; (3) yes, as the specifics a skilled person sees ([[D2-251 Every technique, fitting and thing to build says what it does and why…|D2-251]])

**Default:** the analysis is done in kcal and hours, so the answers only set the conversion

**Zach's answer:** conversation (22 Nov: "Let's do 2 hours")
