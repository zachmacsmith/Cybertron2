## Text
# The calendar

## Summary

A round is a day; the year is compressed, its length a world setting. Daylight, and so the work a day allows, varies by season.
The seasonal stocks run on a second clock scaled by the year's length.

## Decided

- [[D4-13 A round is a day|D4-13]]: a round is a day; the calendar is compressed (year length a world setting, about 16-60 days).
- [[D4-17 Units a day and the action cost table are settled by experiment;|D4-17]]: daylight varies by season (agents/work.md).
- [[D2-214 Two clocks|D2-214]]: two clocks. Day schedules (the weather's events, work, nights) run on the engine day. Seasonal and yearly stocks (soil
  water, groundwater and wells, snowpack, the slow layer) move about 365 ÷ the year's days real days in each engine day.

## Mechanics

- **Seasons**: spring, summer, autumn, winter, of equal length, set by the world (days a season); a world starts at a season and a
  day in it.
- **Cold**: winter is cold; a world may also mark cold days outside winter.
- **Daylight**: the work units of a day follow the season (agents/work.md).

## Parameters

| Parameter | Value | Status |
|---|---|---|
| year length | about 16-60 days | decided ([[D4-13 A round is a day|D4-13]]; a world setting) |
| days a season | a quarter of the year | calibrate |

## What agents see

The season and the day of it ("autumn, day 3 of 6"); the cold in words.
