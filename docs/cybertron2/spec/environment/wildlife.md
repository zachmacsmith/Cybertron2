# Wildlife

## Summary

The wild kinds that live on the land, how they are taken, and the rights in them. The mechanism they live on (a field over the
land, one movement rule, observation as the collapse) is a proposal (proposals/living_populations.md) and not yet decided; until it
is, these rules wait for it.

## Decided

- D2-192: the first wild kinds in four waves.
- D2-204: people take and answer the wild by general methods, with skill by method and familiarity by kind (replaces D2-192's
  own technique and skill per kind).
- D2-205: carcasses and bodies are scavenged; a deep grave draws nothing.
- D2-206: the cat comes by itself or is found.
- D2-193: dangerous kinds can wound or kill the hunter.
- D2-194: rights in wild kinds by law at a world's start; taking against them is poaching.
- D2-195: legendary individuals, rare and named.
- D2-196: hounds in wave 2, hawks in wave 3.
- D2-197: lead times and creature memory in engine days, never less than one.
- D2-198: kinds answer scares in different ways: get used to them, are scared off, leave for good, or do not answer.

## Mechanics

- **The kinds, by wave** (D2-192):

  | Wave | Kinds |
  |---|---|
  | 1 | red deer, hare, trout, eel, salmon, wildfowl, raven |
  | 2 | wolf, wild boar, fox, wild bees |
  | 3 | beaver, otter, pine marten, brown bear, falcon, swan |
  | later | rats and mice in stores; rabbits and warrens; pigeons and dovecotes; fishponds, deer parks, hives |

  Each kind's habitat, movement, season, count, goods and rarity are data (proposals/wildlife.md §4; calibrate).
- **Taking** (D2-204): by general methods (hunt, stalk, drive, snare, net, trap in water, den work, gather young; pest work, guard
  and shut for what raids), each taking what its needs and favours allow among the kinds' traits (size, runs, social, home, water,
  a cue to move); skill is the method's, and a person's familiarity with a kind adds to it for that kind and gates its expert
  readings; methods are known by upbringing and by what the land holds, and learnt as any craft (proposals/methods_and_encounters.md;
  calibrate). Gear, a crew or a trained beast multiply some.
- **Danger** (D2-193): a boar hunt without a crew or hounds, a bear, and a wolf at bay can wound or kill the hunter, by a chance
  that a crew, hounds and gear lower (calibrate).
- **Rights** (D2-194): a world file may give at its start a right in a wild kind or its place (deer and their cover in a wood,
  swans, a fishery or a weir on a stretch of river) to a lord, a household or a village, as a claim (world/holding.md). Taking
  against the right is poaching: possible, and seen by the noticing rule like any other taking.
- **Hounds and hawks** (D2-196): trained beasts held by a household and led along. Hounds come in wave 2. A hound widens the ground
  over which a hunt finds its kind, holds dangerous game at bay (lowering D2-193's danger), courses hares and deer, and guards a
  house or a herd. It eats every day, is bred and trained (a skill), and can be killed by boar or wolves. Hawks come in wave 3: a
  falcon taken young and trained by one person over months, which takes wildfowl and hares for its keeper.
- **Legendary individuals** (D2-195): seldom, a kind present in a world has one named individual (the white hart, the great boar, the
  old dog-wolf), met and remembered as an individual on demand.

- **Time in the wild** (D2-197): a sign leads its event by at least one engine day. A creature's learned response (a field found,
  a scare learned, a fold that fed it, a wood where it was hunted) builds and fades over engine days. Stocks and rates follow the
  year clock (calibrate).
- **Answers to scares** (D2-198): each kind has one of four answers to a harmless scare (a watch, a scarecrow, noise, a light):

  | Answer | What happens | Kinds (first set) |
  |---|---|---|
  | gets used to it | the scare works for a few days, then less, then not at all; a new scare works again | wild boar, woodpigeons, rooks, red deer at the ricks |
  | scared off | the scare drives them off for some days; they come back and the same scare works again | roe deer, hares, wildfowl, fox, wolves while the fold is kept |
  | leaves for good | a scare at the wrong time drives them off for years | a heronry in spring, a beaver family trapped |
  | does not answer | scares do nothing; only traps, cats, stores and timing do | rats and mice, voles, insects, slugs |

  Real harm (a beast killed or hurt, a dog that bites) works on every kind, and makes the kind wary of that place for longer than a
  scare does (calibrate).

## Open

- The mechanism (proposals/living_populations.md).
- Every number (densities, group sizes, skill effects, danger chances): calibrate.

- **Carcasses and bodies** (D2-205): a carcass, and a body unburied or shallowly buried, is food for the wild kinds that scavenge,
  in stages by its size on the day's clock (proposals/carcass_and_decay.md; calibrate); a grave dug to its full units draws nothing.
- **The cat** (D2-206): settles by itself at a building with mice, or is found and brought.
