**The peoples benchmark's worlds** (proposals/peoples_benchmark.md; review 38.69): each model is a people; a people's aim is its living numbers less the other peoples' together; each people starts as about 10, almost all early adults, in a home village of its own; the world is much larger than the homes, its places designed by hand with land types and rare resources so that expanding and intensifying are both open; every home feeds its people comfortably when farmed well (the capacity ladder's "worked" about 2x its people); travel in whole days; long horizons; built on the ecology branch (the wild and water packs)
