**Space inside a parcel is a count, not positions:** a parcel's ground for buildings (and a field's plots) is a number taken and freed, shown to agents as used and free ("the Green: 5 houses, room for 2 more"); things lying there have no position, only how plain or hidden they lie (D4-70)
