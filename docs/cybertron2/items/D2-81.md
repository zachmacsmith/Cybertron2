**Which events have a fuse in the first worlds:** a raid on a building, only sometimes (a chance that anyone detects it at all, from a watcher, dogs, noise heard by neighbours, and how long the structure holds); fire; meetings on the road; finds on the road worth taking (goods, a body, a herd once herds wander). A traveller on the road gets these calls (an exception to D4-10). The fuse's length sets how much the woken can do in their one call (a short fuse one act, a long one several). Arrivals at a shut gate are not a fuse event: the dawn check, then the talk step (D2-5)
