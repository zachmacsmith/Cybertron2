**Calibrate across a distribution of worlds**: weather, the environmental systems and the ecosystems are calibrated on a set of core test worlds covering a diverse range of environments (mountains, coasts, floodplains, dry and wet, cold and mild, and others), not on the few worlds of the moment; the worlds are expected to differ (their weather, their ecosystems); the check is that a calibration generalises in the expected ways and breaks nothing in the extremes, never that the worlds come out alike
