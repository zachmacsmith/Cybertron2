**Whether acts on owned things default to one's own: unnamed, a take from sets, a slaughter, a lift touches only one's own; another's needs naming it (by owner or tag) and is a choice (theft)**: Found 21 Nov: things.find picks the most recently known thing matching a place or set words (the snares, my traps) with no preference for one's own, so a take from my snares can empty a neighbour's; farm._herd_on prefers one's own herd but falls back to anyone's ((own or hs)[0]), so a slaughter with no herd of one's own on the parcel kills a neighbour's beast; stores already refuse (not yours to use). Proposed general rule across sets, herds, piles, plots and buildings. Touches 37.4's things.py and verbs.py and 37.2's farm.py.
