**One fish stock per water body**: a lake's resident fish are one stock, sized by the whole lake and fished from every water parcel on it, and the wild layer's fish in its areas are the same stock; a river's resident fish are one stock per reach (D2-222); the generator writes the water bodies (lakes and reaches: id, area, the areas and parcels on each) and tags each water parcel with its body; the engine holds the stock on the body; a very large lake may later be split into basins
