**Sowing windows are lore, not rules; skill fades slowly and partly; wear in a village's terms** (proposals/economic_calibration.md, History 8 Nov): the agents' rules do not state when crops are sown; a crop sown out of its window is not refused and does not come up (nobody is told why); households holding a field start with the sowing lore as a line kept for good (lore.py), passed on as kept lines are, so it is a farmers' advantage. Skill fades only after half a year unpractised, by half a year of what can be lost, never below 60% of one's best gain, and comes back three times faster while below one's best. Millstones wear after 2000 grain whether an ox or the river turns them; a leat silts in a year; an oven after 10 firings. With it: the walk stays on the day's route (the shortest path joining one's jobs in order, D4-62), at 0.1 of a unit a leg past the first two (was 0.25 a parcel past two: about 0.6 of a unit a person a day in E2's first 14 days, now about 0.13)
