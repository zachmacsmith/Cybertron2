**Remove the engine's automatic detection and telling of breaches and thefts: no line says 'X stole from you' or 'X went against rule n'; people learn of a taking or a breach only by what they sense (someone seen entering and taking, perhaps not known who; the store lower; tracks), and judge it themselves**: Zach, 23 Nov: 'automatically detecting and notifying of breaches should later be removed. It shouldn't tell someone they have been stolen from. Maybe they see the person enter and take stuff and they don't know who it is but it makes no sense to say XYZ stole from you because that is it deciding the physics of something that really lives in the social physics which is different.' To audit: the book's going-against lines (book._against and its morning lines, u6 §7, D2-274's 'going against a household rule is recorded and noticed'), a set's robbed_by and any result naming who took from it (placed.py), the thefts record shown to people (verbs.py take, today['thefts']); the store-theft signs (signs.py THEFT) already describe only what is seen.
