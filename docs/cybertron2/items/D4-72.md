**No sign asserts an act or a feeling the person did not choose:** signs of a person show their body (hunger, injury, age, pregnancy, what they carry or lead) and what they did; their behaviour and feelings come from their own replies. The bereavement signs ("was heard weeping", "goes about grey-faced and silent") and "is short with everyone" are removed. Signs about those who live in a building apply only to a building with beds
