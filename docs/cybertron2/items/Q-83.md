**(Closed 22 Nov: D2-284.)** **Whether the smoke racks hold a real hut's load (about 500 fish on 2 spaces) with a 1-night keeping smoke, so a glut is limited by hands and wood, not the hut**: smokehouse_capacity.md (22 Nov): the racks hold about 40 kg on 3 spaces (about 2.7 kg a m2), a tenth to a twentieth of a real hut; 3 nights is about a month real, two to three times a keeping smoke. Proposed: 250 fish a space, the fitting 2 spaces holding 500; 1 night a lot; 1 unit to hang 30 fish; 1 firewood per 60 fish; tending 1 unit a night (a named departure). Needs the fitting's own holds (keeping.py). The hearth rack's 3 nights need the same check. (replaced by D2-291)
