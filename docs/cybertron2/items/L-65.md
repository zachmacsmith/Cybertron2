**A plan revised in the morning talk drops `come`.** The meeting step reads `come` from the latest actions; one who answers a morning message restates the day's plan, often without `come`, and is left out of a meeting it meant to attend (Walter, harm probe, twice: "Go to the meeting about watching doors"). Keep `come` unless the person says it will not go, or remind the talk prompt that the plan holds it
