**Crops grow from the day they are sown, and the cold is the season's limit:** a crop still growing when winter comes dies unless it is hardy; crops are grain, winter grain (hardy), legumes (restore the soil, less food), a garden (fast, spoils) and hay (fodder); the same crop twice on a plot is likelier to fail
