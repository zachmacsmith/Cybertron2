**Every perceived line about the world comes from the one noticing rule:** a sign row sets how plain it is (own), its sense, its conditions and its scope, never a chance; the chance is the base chance x own x visibility for the sense x presence x attention x the eye (sight only), capped after the base chance. Sound and smell carry by steps in the parcel graph (presence at least the carry for the steps, times the sense's visibility: weather, cover, night, masking); touch only where one is; reach is shorthand (near: the spot, attention x2; around: the spot and next door). Things, concerned lines (in full only when it happened to one or in one's house), dawn lines, the attended subject (attention x5, presence at least 0.5), the place's ambient lines, what one worked on (attention x3), glimpses, feeling watched and road notices all go through it.
