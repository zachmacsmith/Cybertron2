**The carrying burden is an incomplete measure** (economy.py; 02zl §7). It assumes the short village carries everything itself, on foot, with empty return trips; it counts any ox as one ox, with no harness check, and ignores handcarts. Better: the share of the region's labour spent on transport, whoever carries, with two-way loads, reported on foot, with what the world holds, and with an ox cart or a trader. A warning only (D2-190)
