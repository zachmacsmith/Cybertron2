**Two clocks** (proposals/environment_systems.md; docs/review/37_growth/02zzn_environment_prototype.md): the world runs one clock on day schedules (the weather's events: a storm's rain, a cold snap, wind; work and nights) and one on seasonal and yearly scales, set by how many days the world's year has: the seasonal stocks (soil water, groundwater and wells, snowpack, the slow layer) move about 365 ÷ the year's days real days in each engine day, so a summer draws the soil down and a winter fills it whatever the year's length
