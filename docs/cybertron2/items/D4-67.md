**Setting off in the morning or at nightfall:** in the morning, as the day's first act, one walks the day (no work) and comes in by nightfall of the road's last day, with a night out for each day between, and spends that evening at the place one reaches; at nightfall (the default), one works the day, sleeps out, and comes in at midday with half that day's units.
