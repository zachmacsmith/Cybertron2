**Shelter and fire are separate:** where one sleeps (out, bedroll, tent, a lean-to camp, a house) sets the night's food; a fire there cooks, and in the cold warms; a fire in the open burns 2 timber (D4-49), a camp's 1, a house's by its hearth (0.5, 2 cold); a fire in the open or a camp's is **shared by up to 4** sleeping there; a fire ring in a house half-warms 6 spaces, a hearth fully warms 12
