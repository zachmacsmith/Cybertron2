**Standing rules are written in a stated grammar the prompt gives in full (each kind and its fields: rule, goods, to / from / in, qty, by, when, unless, words); the engine reads the fields, not the words; 'words' is the person's own sentence, kept and shown beside the engine's reading; a rule missing a field is refused saying exactly which field and its form. This replaces D2-274's rule set 'in the agent's own sentence' and the word-reading added in W-102.**
