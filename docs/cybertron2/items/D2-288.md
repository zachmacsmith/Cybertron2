**Bulk goods are counted in handling units of a stated weight and every recipe is written in real kilograms: timber and stone a 25 kg load, firewood a 10 kg bundle, straw a 16 kg truss, hay 10 kg, charcoal 20 kg, withies and bark 10 kg bundles.**
