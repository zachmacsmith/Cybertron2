**Risks attach to things:** every kind of object (a building, a fitting such as a mill's wheel, a store, a herd, a crop, a road, a mine) can carry its own risks in its data, raised by neglect (missed upkeep) and by conditions; new objects bring their own risks; incidents also emerge from the systems (a neglected house collapses and injures those inside; a mill's wheel breaks or is damaged in a flood)
