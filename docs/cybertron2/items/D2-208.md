**The world keeps the land it runs on** (proposals/land_kept.md): the generated land at its full grain (the 150 m cells and every field the generator made of them) is part of the world, not thrown away after the summaries are drawn; it goes with the world file, into every run's directory and its published record, is loaded by the engine at build, and the run's changes to the land are kept on top of it; a run is reproducible from what it kept, not by regenerating. Zach: "let's actually store and build upon the world we are running in. Even just for reproducibility purposes WTF would we throw it away?"
