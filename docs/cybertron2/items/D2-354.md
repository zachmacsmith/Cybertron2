**The architecture is a healthy balance, not a strict one: most of the world is data acting on general, shared primitives that are extended when a new feature needs it; a system may own its specifics where they truly belong to it; the aim is that the engine can change quickly without falling into a tangle of ad hoc fixes or becoming so rigid that ad hoc fixes are the only fast way to build. Refines D2-353.**
