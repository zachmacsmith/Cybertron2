**Shorter waits for children, a higher chance:** pregnancy **2 days** and the gap after a birth **1 day** (at the default lifespan; scaled, D2-153), and conception **0.8 x (1 - e^(-0.5 x units))** a night (about 0.31 for 1 unit each, 0.51 for 2, 0.62 for 3); as built a population could not replace itself at any effort (02ze: R0 0.59 trying every night); now about 3 births a woman trying every night, replacement at a little over half the nights. Limits should be soft (feeding, the vulnerable young) rather than hard waits (proposals/fertility.md §4, §5, open)
