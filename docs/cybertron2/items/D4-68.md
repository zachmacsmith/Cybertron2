Anyone may name a tagged thing (`name {thing, as}`); the name is known to the namer, everyone at the place and the namer's household, who read "(H4: Old Brock)" and may use the name. People, places, parcels and buildings keep their own names; goods are quantities. Individual beasts not now. A world setting (`tags: true`)
