**Roads are mostly a day long:** the generator joins neighbouring places by roads of about one day; a two-day road is rare (where the land gives nowhere to stop: a moor, a pass); a longer journey passes through places on the way (D4-10's waypoints), so a route of three days is three roads; where a day's road would cross wild land with no settlement, the generator puts a stopping place there (a hamlet where the site allows, else a wild stop at a spring, a ford or a meadow: proposed); the default region is about 50 km square for 4 settlements (amends D2-181)
