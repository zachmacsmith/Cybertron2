**The economy check counts crops that winter will clear.** landgen/economy.py counts every standing crop whose `ripe_day` falls within the run, but the engine clears unripe crops when winter begins. The takeover world (38.59) passed with food 881 of 720; its Bottom Field (oats, ripe day 34) was cleared on day 14, and both 60-day runs starved from day 20. Proposed: count a crop only if it ripens before the next winter's start; also count stores lost to spoilage out of doors. The same world's 881 also held 206 from 'fields after a year' (every field resown and reaped in full by day 60: in the run, three plots of 15 to 18 were sown and the last was cleared by the second winter a day before it ripened) and 131 from the woods' flow; store and reapable crops were 383, about 29 days for 13. A crop's `ripe_day` past the next winter should also be refused when the world is written: the engine recorded this one as sown on day 26, in the future. 7 Nov (37.4): cybertron2/capacity (D4-102) now gives the land's ratio by level, the buffer, the first own harvest and the crops the frost takes first, from the engine's own rules; proposed that the economy check call it (capacity.model.analyse, run_horizon) in place of its fixed numbers
