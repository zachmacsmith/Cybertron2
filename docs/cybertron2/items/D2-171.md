**Extravagance in tiers:** a building or monument may be built plain, fine, rich or grand, each tier dearer (materials x1.5, x2.5, x4; work x2, x4, x8: calibrate) and more noticeable (fine: seen from neighbouring parcels; rich: named in the village and arrival overviews; grand: seen from the road's last stretch coming in); a tier can be raised later by paying the difference
