**Each morning a person is told what they ate the night before: which goods and how much, from which store, whether by a rule (which one) or by the default order, and how well it met their hunger (full, short, still hungry); and what the household drew from its store for the meal, so a store going down at the meal is seen as eaten, not as vanished.**
