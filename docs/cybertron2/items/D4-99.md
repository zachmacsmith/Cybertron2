**Graves dug by many, over days; a cairn after burial; signs of an unfinished grave:** each bury act puts in up to 2 units, kept on the body as a share of the whole grave (soft 2, stony 4, frozen 6, reckoned on the ground that day), adding up across diggers and days; a cairn (3 stone, 2 units) can be raised over a grave after the burial; an unfinished grave is seen by how far it is dug, and as frozen again, holding snow or water, or left untouched
