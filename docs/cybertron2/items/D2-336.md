**A shelf life is stated one of two ways: 'keeps for d days' (preserved goods: about a quarter lost by then, D2-289) or 'goes off after d days' (fresh, perishable food: about half gone by then, so fresh fish and meat go off quickly after a few nights, as before the rebuild); both on the keeping curve's nights.**
