**Who hears and who is woken** (refines D2-7 and D4-15): a message to one person reaches them before work and **wakes** them (they are asked again and may answer or change their plan); speech **in a building** reaches **everyone with access to it who is at the node that day** (a house: its household), wherever they are at the time; speech **to the town** reaches everyone at the node; neither wakes anyone. Answers go back the same way, in a few waves (cybertron 1's DM step)
