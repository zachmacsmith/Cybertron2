**D2-306 as meant: material got at the site of a work (field stone, brush, turf, daub) is not a material to bring; the work of getting it is added to the work's units when its cost is calculated. The stage 1 build removed such material without adding its work (the cairn, the wattle roof), which is a fault, not the rule.**
