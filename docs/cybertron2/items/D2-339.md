**The household book's grammar: five kinds and drop. set (name; people, goods or places); keep (goods; in?; at least?: where a good lives, and a floor not eaten or used); first (goods as an ordered list); share (order? of people; who?; qty?; goods?: who is fed first, rations, who gets which food); do (who; act: any act; when?; unless?: every chore, move, tend, hang, salt, fetch, mend, and fallbacks, a fallback being a do with no condition); drop (n). Conditions are structured: an amount (goods, in, under / over / full / room), a process (in, state: done / in process / fire out), arrives, season, seen (a named sign set), a person (starving, home). A rule is a record in its own reply field 'rules', free, any time or in the evening, with for, words and n; own before household, one named person or good before a set, newer before older. Rules between households, store access and timed acts are out.**
