**Travel decides itself most of the time:** minor trouble on the road is resolved automatically, by the traveller's choices at departure ("if there is trouble, turn back", "keep pushing") or a default, and reported on arrival with what it cost; there is still a chance of a fuse call, and more extreme or more complex situations (challenges that interact, other people, a serious risk) give one, with more choices (within D2-80)
