Since they could plausibly catch hundreds] raise both: the runs to a real rivers (3 to 6 times, salmon up the river) and the gears holds to real gear in kg (a trap about 10 kg, a weir section about 100 kg on a peak night, nets when built; 3 to 5 times today), with the binding limit moved to what follows the catch (fresh fish spoils in a day or two; gutting work, salt about 1 kg per 4 to 5 kg of fish, rack and smokehouse space, fuel), so a run is a glut and the question is what can be kept; earlier text: the weir does not pay because the runs are too small (eels 2.5 to 7 times below a real river's, salmon 20 to hundreds of times, spawning only in head waters): runs 3 to 6 times larger, salmon spread up the river by km, the weir's hold stated in kg (about 30 kg), fewer and denser run nights; (d) fishing worlds lose about half their fish food: resident stocks about 2.2 times; (e) a master hunter falls from 2.5 to 1.9 food a unit against plough farming's 2.4, still nine people's food on a day's hunting, so D2-255's disproportion holds against need, not against farming
