**Who stole is known only to those who saw it:** a theft names the thief only to its witnesses (those at home, watching the house, or by the noticing rule's small chance nearby), never by chance afterwards; the house's people are not told a loss: they find it as anything is found, by the store's signs (D4-74: less than there was, footprints in snow or mud) and its count when they are home; others learn who did it only if a witness tells them
