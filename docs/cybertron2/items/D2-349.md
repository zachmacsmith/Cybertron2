**Founders' seeded lines about other households follow where they live and what is known: households in one's own village get the neighbour lines; households in other villages one's household knows of get lines of their own kind (that village, not a neighbour's); villages one's household does not know of get no seeded lines. Built in stage 5b part 3.**
