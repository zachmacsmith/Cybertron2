**An unnamed act on several of one's own things at a place: an act that takes what things yield and leaves them as they were, cheap to undo or repeat (tending traps, snares, nets), applies to all of one's own of that kind there; an act that uses up, kills, destroys or gives away a thing (slaughtering from a herd, taking from a pile, giving, dismantling) needs a named target and is otherwise refused, listing the tags.**
