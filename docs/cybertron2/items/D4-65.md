**An evening at the hearth, and one wave of answers:** after the day's work, before the day's settling and the night, everyone at a place is asked once where they will sleep (their house, the house they mean to sleep in, or the open), with how their day went and who is with them; what is said there is heard free by everyone at that hearth; a private word may go to anyone at the place; then one wave, in which only those asked something directly (told something, named in what was said at their hearth, a request waiting on them) answer; what is said reaches the rest next morning. The morning keeps one wave of answers (two before). Who hears is fixed by where one is when speaking. A world setting (`evening: true`)
