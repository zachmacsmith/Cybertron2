**The weir in sections, capped, with upkeep by the river** (proposals/river_and_water.md §3b): one weir per water parcel; weirs on a stretch take in turn from upstream; built and upgraded in sections (a wattle first section as built, up to three timber sections, stone footing a section at a time; upgrading costs the difference); a night's catch is the share passing capped by what the weir holds (about 10 food a night for one section, 30 for a full weir); a year's take about 13% of a village of 15's food for one section and 40% for a full weir; wear per section by material x water type x the flow (from the catchment's rain until a river level exists) x the gap shut in high water x ice, so a full weir costs a quarter to a third of its building a year to keep; keeping the catch (smokehouses, salt, fuel) is the intended bottleneck.
