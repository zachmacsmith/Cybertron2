**A field carries one crop for each holding on it:** with plot shares (D2-163), each holder's plots in a field carry their own crop (kind, seed, broken share, weeding, fertility), so one field can hold barley on the Reeds' plots and beans on the Coles'; the field stays one place and one line; a herd on the field tramples or grazes whatever stands there, so a common rotation comes from custom or agreement, never a rule
