**Every act and every technique a person can take is registered with required descriptions: its interface (what it does, how it is said, its arguments and every option, its cost) and what it needs, shown to each person at the detail their familiarity gives; an act or technique without them is a build error. Shapes organise: every act is listed under its shape on a line of its own, never folded into another act's sentence. Interface is never left to memory; the world is.**
