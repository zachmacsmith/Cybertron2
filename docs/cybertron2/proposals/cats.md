# Cats that choose where to live

**Status:** discussion (9 Nov). Nothing decided or built. Refines D2-206 ("the cat comes by itself or is found") as built in brief 2.
**Owner:** 37.2 (author; the wild pack's data). Kept beasts with homes and choices are 37.4's husbandry (proposals/husbandry.md,

## Summary

A cat is an animal that lives where it does best, not goods that settles in a store. It lives **in the wood** (a feral or wild
cat on voles and birds) or **at a building** (on the mice and rats of its store and whatever is left out for it), chooses each
season by food and safety, and **leaves** a house where the mice are gone and no one feeds it. People draw one in by **leaving food
out** (milk, offal, scraps put at the house, which also draws rats and the fox), take a stray found on a hunt or a foray, and give
away kittens. A cat brought in stays only while the house suits it. Cats are rare: a store with mice gains one about once in six
seasons, more if fed.

## The proposal

1. **Where a cat lives.** A small population of cats in each village's ring (the ecology's areas; feral and wild cats together,
   on voles, mice and small birds). A cat at a building is one taken from it; a cat that leaves a building goes back to it.
2. **Its choice, each season's turn** (keyed): a cat in the ring settles at the building that scores best, if any beats the wood:
   mice and rats in the store (the loads that follow the harvest) plus food left out there in the last days (milk, meat, offal, fish
   lying at the house, D2-85) minus a dog fitting (or a dog led there) minus being chased or harmed there (remembered for a season).
   A cat at a building moves on when another scores clearly better, and back to the wood when its house scores below the wood (the
   mice gone, nothing left out). Settling chance at the best house about 0.15 a season with mice, 0.4 if food was left out
   (calibrate; brief 2's 0.25 settled half the stores at once).
3. **First step, without husbandry:** keep the cat as goods in the store (as built) and add only (a) the lower settling rate,
   (b) food left out as the lure, (c) leaving when the store has had no mice and no food left out for a season, (d) a stray found on
   a hunt or foray. That is data and a rule on the existing cat.
4. **Later, with husbandry (K6):** the cat as a kept beast with a home it chooses, kittens each spring (given away, or left to go
   wild), a bond with whoever feeds it, and its own risks (the fox, dogs, traps set for rats).
5. **Food left out draws others too:** the same food that lures a cat is a target for rats, the fox and a dog (brief 2's targets,
   brief 3's scavenging): putting milk out for a cat is a choice with a cost.

## Why

- Zach (9 Nov): "cats should not just settle at stores, but choose to either live elsewhere in the woods, and maybe you can leave
  out foods for a cat".
- Brief 2's gate (OPEN Q-37 d): cats settled at half the stores at the first season's turn.

## Alternatives considered
- **The cat as a fitting or goods only** (as built): simple, but it cannot leave or be drawn, and it settles everywhere at once.
