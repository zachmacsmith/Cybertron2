=== DAY 0 MORNING ===
## Kept for good
- The year turns in four seasons of 8 days: spring, summer, autumn, winter. Summer days are longest for work, winter's shortest. What land and water give changes each season; one season's emptiness says nothing of the next.
- Winter is the hungry time: the wild little, a cold night burns about three times the fuel. What a house eats and burns through winter must be in by autumn's end.
- Fresh meat and fish go off in a night or two, faster in the hand; dry grain and beans keep more than a year. Dried, smoked or salted, food keeps for seasons. Raw food feeds about half what cooked does.
- A grown person eats about one food a night, more after a hard day or a night in the cold. A household of 5 wants about 32 food to see a winter through, and more if the frost comes early.
- What I know of a craft I got by watching, by trying and failing, and by being taught; a day beside one who knows is worth many tries alone.
- My parents, Peter and Nichola, built our house at Eelmouth, and I was born in it.
