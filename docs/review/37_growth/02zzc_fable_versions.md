# 02zzc: Fable 5.2, Fable 5.1 and Opus 5.5 in the same seats, with a full LLM cast (9 Nov)

Rounds 2 and 3 of the paired probes (02zzb was round 1): run 6's Meadcombe (`wild_probe6.json`, branch `ecology`, seed 7,
spring, **6 days**), Ivy (Holt) and Petronel (Carter) as the test seats, swapped between the two arms of each scenario; every
other agent an LLM (Haiku 5.5; Osric Sonnet 5.5 with an aim to hire labour for grain in s1). Round 2 put Fable 5.2 in the Fable
seat, round 3 Fable 5.1, everything else the same. Scenarios as in 02zzb (s1 bargain, s2 raiders, s3 full store next door).
Cost: round 2 $16.86, round 3 $24.71.

**Both Fables against Opus:** more acts, more kinds of act, more messages. **Fable 5.2 against 5.1:** the same activity, but 5.1
writes much longer, more narrative reasons ("I will not watch my family die beside a herd"; "I name Thomasin heir, which is long
overdue"; "where I am best"), is refused about three times as often, never once wrote to Osric, and costs 1.8 times as much (4
times Opus). 5.2 is terser and turns outward: in s1 b, after killing a Wood cow by mistake it owned it and paid in meat, hides and
a cow (Osric accepted), then carried dried meat and coin to Brookford to trade for seed and offered its reaping "to mend the old
quarrel".

**Outcomes:** seven agreements formed in round 2 (Opus Ivy bought grain from Osric and meat from Ivo; Fable 5.2 Ivy bought
firewood three times), one in round 3. One theft in all: Opus Ivy took 1.1 firewood of the Woods' lying on the Green (round 3,
s2 b). Osric never hired labour despite his aim. The fox came to Carter House twice in every run and took nothing (on this seed
its first two visits fail; it took hens on the third in run 6), and no head shut the hens in, so defence is still untested. Ivy
and Alys died on day 6 in every run (OPEN Q-35).

**Limits:** one seed, 6 days, six seats a model; the style differences (activity, reason length, cost) hold in every seat, the
rest is suggestive. Agreements and thefts are counts for the whole run, shaped by the other agents too.
