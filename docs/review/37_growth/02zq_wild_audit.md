## 2. One-off pieces that break the general rules

| # | What I built | What is wrong | The general rule it should be |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | night acts ("watch the field", "pick slugs by lantern") as two day units with a night effect | no night is spent: no sleep lost, no food (the engine's `guard` costs `guard_food`), no light; "by lantern" names a lantern that does not exist; the fire watch burns no wood | **one night commitment** shared with `guard` and the night watch defence.md leaves to Zach (D2-21): it costs the night (no sleep, more food, tired next day), needs light from a thing the state holds and uses it up (a fire burns firewood; a rushlight tallow; a horn lantern a candle), and its effect depends on the light |
| 2 | scares named as strings (fire, clapper, horn, dog, herding) | clappers and horns are not items; the dog watch does not check that the household keeps a dog | a watch's **scare from what is there**: light (a fire), noise (an item that makes it), a dog (the dog fitting or a beast), people; each kind answers by the scare's properties |
| 3 | hens as a house's stock item, six seeded a house with beds, laying five eggs a day in wild.py | kept animals are not beasts; laying is a hard-coded production rule; who keeps hens is not the generator's choice | hens as **kept beasts** (husbandry, 37.4) with production by the same rule as milk; the generator decides who keeps them |
| 4 | "shut the hens in" | an act for one target | **a building's or pen's door state at night** (shut, open), read by every group (a fox at the hens, wolves at a fold, a thief) |
| 5 | group targets as three branches in code (crop, herd, hens) | each new target needs new code | a target as **a parcel stock, a herd's beasts or a container's item**, with what is taken by the kind's data |
| 6 | signs' thresholds and some numbers in code (eaten shares 0.002 and 0.01, hunt notices' 0.15, NOTICE_MOST, the bridge's cap 0.5, RAIN_MEAN 3.4) | numbers belong in data; RAIN_MEAN repeats the climate's own mean | constants in the data tables (NOTICING, the kinds); the climate's mean read from the climate |
| 7 | the mast's signs as their own function (season windows, level comparisons) | the pulsed-resource archetype is general | **a pulse's signs** by its state (flowering, set, fallen, failed), for any pulse |
| 8 | presentation spots (the first forest parcel for presence, mast, loads) | a presentation choice not named as one | named as a choice, kept deterministic, documented |
| 9 | the herd's `ill` record, and herd deaths through `set` on 37.4's herd fields | a field on another session's record, not in the schema | the herd schema (37.4) to carry a beast's state, or the beasts entity |
| 10 | murrain meat edible with no effect | a diseased carcass is food like any other | blocked on illness in people |
