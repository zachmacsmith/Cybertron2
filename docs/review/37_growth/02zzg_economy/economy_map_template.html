What the network shows

  • The core is a web. Timber feeds 32 acts, fittings and uses, and stone 17. Ore needs charcoal, which needs timber; copper makes tools, coin and the plough; hides make harness, packs and tents; salt feeds three ways of keeping food.
  • The wild layer is mostly leaves. The wild plant foods, nuts and eggs go only to eating. Antler goes only to a horn, and a horn only scares. Tallow goes only to lights.
  • 29 goods have a single use, and four have none: castoreum, beaver pelt and offal serve no one (rotting meat is waste by design). Antler and the hen come from nowhere the tables name; both exist only when a world seeds them.
  • Few chains run more than two steps outside metal and bread. Most goods are one act from the land and one step from being eaten.
