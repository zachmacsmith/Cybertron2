# 02zzk: where to draw the border for now: the medieval village and what the full map says to take from it (15 Nov 2026)

## Read this first: this is a map, not a plan

Zach (15 Nov): "I think this is like building a map of all of the future to come back and have better clarity of where to draw
the borders for now. But make sure to note that down clearly so I don't accidentally scope creep into trying to build the entirety
of human history."

- **The 02zzj research folder and the Flint to Fab web are a map of everything an economy could become.** They exist so that the
  border for the current engine can be drawn with the whole territory in view. **They are not a backlog.** Nothing in them is
  proposed for building because it appears there.
- **The border is the medieval village and its market town,** in the engine's current form: one-day places, households of agents,
  32 to 64 day lives, runs of 100 to 200 days (part 09 §1). Things past the border are kept as map only.
- **Something crosses the border only when a research question needs it** (a benchmark arm, a probe, a mechanism a run shows to be
  missing), and then through the normal path: a proposal, Zach's decision, a brief.
- **The first bottleneck is not missing content.** In the peoples run (pw1), people starved next to their neighbours' dried meat,
  meat rotted at doors and the stone on Coppice Hill was never reached. Agents do not yet use what exists well, and the social
  side ([[L-106 MAJOR what the engine remembers and settles for agents, and sepa|L-106]]) is the gate to every layer past the village (part 09 §9). New content comes after, or alongside, those.

## 1. How to judge what to take

A thing earns a place inside the border if most of these hold:

1. **It makes dependence or leverage:** a chokepoint someone can hold, a conversion chain, enforcement that needs people
   (`scenario_design.md` §1).
2. **It is rows, not code:** it fits the existing handlers for gathering, making and building (part 09 §1, "the world grows by
   data").
3. **An agent can see it and act on it** within its prompt: a sign worth acting on, an option it knows (senses guide; knowledge.md).
4. **It is a lever for a benchmark:** growth of a line or a people needs it (descendants and peoples benchmarks).
5. **It fits the time scale:** it plays out within a life or a run, or is inherited through lore.
6. **It crosses another chain** at a shared bottleneck (the woods, the river, land near the village, the thresholds: economy_web
   §3). The research's clearest finding is that chains merge where they share an intermediate (02zzj README, finding 3).

## 2. The tiers

Status: **E** in the engine on `main`; **W** on `peoples-worlds`; **P** proposed (economy_web.md stages, briefs); **new** suggested
by the full map and not yet proposed anywhere. Parts are the 02zzj parts the reason comes from.

### Inside the border, built

Grain, flour and bread with the mill and oven; fields, soil, manure and crops by season; herds, milk and cheese; hunting (4c) and
basic fishing; forage and the wild ecology (W); woods, firewood, timber, charcoal; copper smelting and coin at the anvil; building
with rooms and fittings; the pack, ox and handcart on roads; offers, meetings and goods credit; households, claims and heirs;
births, care, coming of age; combat, raids, guards, walls and gates (spec/conflict).

### Discovery: inside the border, small

Know-how stays in the engine; knowing-that stays with the agents. A dozen techniques with small grids of choices (sowing,
malting, tanning, smelting, kiln firing, hay, cheese), a few leaps through surprising signs, local facts to find, and one probe that
asks whether stronger models experiment better (this session's discussion; OPEN [[L-103 Crafting and discovering new things|L-103]]). After [[L-106 MAJOR what the engine remembers and settles for agents, and sepa|L-106]].

### At the border: the market town, later

Cloth with clothing (wool, spinning, weaving, the fulling mill on the same stream as the mill); carts on tracks; boats on rivers;
candles and soap; the deer park and warren; gardens and orchards; guilds; a chartered market with its court. Each needs either a
need the engine lacks (clothing), an edge kind (carts, boats), or institutions (guilds, charters). Economy_web stage 5.

### Past the border: map only

Merchant networks and long-distance trade; banking, bills of exchange and paper money; putting-out at scale; states, taxation and
war finance; glass, lenses, clocks and printing; gunpowder; everything from mineral energy on (coal, steam, rail, chemicals,
electricity, oil, computing). These need representation the engine does not have (people who are not agents, populations by
place, a hierarchy of places: part 09 additions 6 to 8) or eras a run never reaches.

## 3. Whole domains the map is missing

The 02zzj parts cover goods, farming, crafts, exchange, labour and institutions, societies, the industrial revolution, later
paradigms, prehistory, world economies, cities and modern industries. Counting mentions across the parts shows these as thin or
absent. Each is marked by whether it bears on the border now.

| Domain | Why it is economic | In the parts | Bears on the border now? |
|---|---|---|---|
| **War and force** | demand that drove metal and precision; states and public debt made to pay for war; feudal tenure as land for armed service; tribute, plunder and slaving; arms chains through things the engine has (horn, sinew, dung for saltpetre) | scattered; "knight", "vassal" or "fief" once in 13 parts | **yes**: lordship and enforcement rest on it |
| **Knowledge-making** | instruments, records, patronage, secrets and apprenticeship; later academies, patents and laboratories | parts 07 and 08 only | **yes**: [[L-103 Crafting and discovering new things|L-103]] |
| **Religion and ritual** | temples as the first stores and banks; tithe; monasteries as landlords and innovators; feasts and pilgrimage as demand | temple economies (06, 10); little else | **yes**: the tithe store, feasts |
| **The household's own work, care and kinship** | child-rearing, cooking and nursing as most of all labour; dowry, bride-price and inheritance as transfers of capital | almost absent | **yes**: children and heirs already exist |
| **Health and medicine** | epidemics set wages; herbal remedies and midwifery; the urban graveyard | famine and plague (05, 06); medicine thin | partly: disease by host and area exists (wave 4) |
| **Environment and depletion** | soil exhaustion, woodland loss, salinisation, smoke; limits that turned societies | scattered | partly: the ecology and soil exist |
| **Luxury, status and feasting** | most long-distance trade was luxuries; the consumer revolution pulled the industrial one; feasts as politics | thin | partly: extravagance tiers of building exist |
| **Crime and predation** | theft, banditry, piracy, smuggling, fraud: the costs enforcement exists to cut | almost absent | partly: raids exist |
| **Measurement and standards** | weights and measures, their fraud and their regulation | Indus weights, assizes; scattered | yes, with records and the balance |

The web also lacks links between civilisations (gunpowder leads nowhere; Islamic credit does not reach European bills; Indian cotton
does not reach Lancashire's spinning): a linking pass would fix that (02zzj visual README).

## 4. What would change this border

- A run showing a missing mechanism binds: agents trying to do something the engine cannot express.
- A benchmark arm or probe that needs a layer: a manor world for [[L-106 MAJOR what the engine remembers and settles for agents, and sepa|L-106]], a discovery probe for [[L-103 Crafting and discovering new things|L-103]].
- Representation arriving (rule-run households, populations by place): that moves the market town and proto-industry inside.

Until then, the map is for looking things up, not for building from.
