# 38.74: The lake world's first live run ([[W-50 The lake world's live run Meresby, 14 people, 14 days ($11.05)|W-50]]): what came of the water

Session 37.4, 16 Nov 2026. World: `cybertron2/specs/water/lake.yaml` (37.2's, seed 3, standard settings added), on `ecology-water`
at 39c1a6d. Meresby: 14 people (3 Opus, 2 Sonnet, 6 Haiku, 3 children), 14 days from summer day 4. It cost $11.05. Everyone lived;
no meal was missed and there were no errors. The run is in `runs/water_lake_r1` (stopped once by a container restart after day 2,
resumed from the journal).

## Fishing

- **The Brook** (Meresby's water, small):
  - fished by hand 89 times for 21.4 fish, about 11% of the run's meals;
  - the catch fell from about 3 a day (days 0 to 4) to 0.5 a day by day 10 as the Brook was fished out ("the Brook is spent and winter
    is close", Edric, day 12);
  - who fished: Haiku adults (Rose 39 acts, Felice 22, Edric 12, Drew 11). The Opus heads directed and fished three times.
- **The eel run** came once, on day 6, a rain night:
  - the sign was noticed ("the eels are running at the Brook tonight"), and four people fished it by hand: 19 eels;
  - wicker traps were set four times (days 4, 6, 7, 8) and caught 4 eels on nights 6 to 8;
  - **none was lifted.** Rose and Drew tried `take` with `"item": "fish"` and were refused ("no fish lying there"), because an eel is
    stored as meat, not fish. [[D2-216 Eels and salmon are fish|D2-216]], now being built, fixes it;
  - the storms then wrecked the traps ("The eel traps are fouled and broken with nothing in them", Felice, day 9).
- **The rod:**
  - Hawise wanted to angle, but making a rod needs 0.1 hide, and the hares they took gave none they could keep;
  - Felice and Edric asked Baldwin for a hide. No rod was made.
- **The mere** (about 12.9 km² of lake in Meresby's ring) was never fished:
  - the village has no parcel on it, and fishing works only at a parcel, so the lake world's lake cannot be fished from the
    village;
  - fowl were seen on it six times ("duck and geese on the open water"), and nobody netted: no one knows `net`.
- **Not built:** no weir, no smokehouse. Both crafts are on the everyone-knows line (proposals/livelihood_archetypes.md).
- **Salt:** salting fish was talked of from day 0 ("eat it first or we salt it"), but there is no salt in this world. One try at
  salting meat was refused for want of meat.
- **Drying:** a calf was slaughtered and dried at two hearths (8 and 2 meat: the one-batch rule).
- **Heron, beaver, otter:** nothing perceived or done.

## Findings

1. **Eels could not be lifted by `take fish`** ([[D2-216 Eels and salmon are fish|D2-216]] fixes this; a lift with no item named would have worked).
2. **The lake can't be reached by fishing:** a village's water in its ring has no parcel to fish from (37.2's world and 37.4's
   methods; to raise with 37.2).
3. **The Brook empties in about ten days of four people fishing.** It is the village's only fish water.
4. **The weir and the smokehouse go unused** while everyone "knows" them (the archetypes proposal).
