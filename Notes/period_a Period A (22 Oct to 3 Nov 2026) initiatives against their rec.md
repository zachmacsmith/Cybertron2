  days, which were handed to 37.4 as packages. What each package left out went into its build note or brief and stopped there:
  38.24 §5 and 02zh "Not in this package". Examples are node gates, the node raid, ledgers, specialised tools, room bookings,
  village walls and towers. The spec text reads as if these rules hold. A decision row has no "built" column, so nothing flags the
  gap.
- **Running documents die at the first pivot.** Three running records stopped within a day, when space became a new engine (22 Oct
  19:47) and the work was handed to 37.4 (21:00): the work log Zach asked for, the board Status row, and the porting ledger. Until
  WORK.md (9 Nov) there was no ledger of work in flight.
- **A switch of direction closes nothing behind it.** Post 1's couplings contract was never answered or marked superseded when the
  old engine was frozen. Package E was marked deferred, but the old-engine designs 02a to 02e only say so in 02f.
- **The last asks before a gap get lost.** The generator critique of 3 Nov 04:12 and 04:20 was the last thing before a 38-hour gap.
  The next session opened on fishing (4 Nov 18:21), and the critique has no commit or row.
- **Chat-only answers.** The 1984-bench check (24 Oct) and the "what is left / has it been specced" lists (23 Oct 04:22, 04:34;
  1 Nov 22:23) were answered in conversation. Only some of the findings became rows ([[Q-12 Decisions made with 37.2 that belong in 37.4's spec areas and are|Q-12]] did; [[L-74 What Cybertron 2 lacks for a society richer than Cybertron 1's|L-74]] came 16 days later, from
  another session).
- **Deferrals reach OPEN only when Zach says "flag".** Every L-row of the period quotes him. A deferral the assistant recommends
  stays in the proposal's Status or Open questions and never gets a row: the planet, wider environments, generator questions 6 and
  7, fertility questions 2 to 4. Zach had to ask "where are you flagging everything btw?" (23 Oct 20:13) before the flagged table
  existed (c3552c5).
- **Gaps left inside a document for Zach to fill are never raised as asks.** 02zd carries seven "[reasoning not documented — Zach
  to fill in]" markers that no OPEN row points to.
- **Statuses lag builds done by the other session.** 37.4 built 37.2's designs but did not touch 37.2's proposals, which still say
  "Design only". Spec write-ups owed across sessions pile up as paused rows that are "not begun": [[W-9 Compile the generator into specworldgeneration.md (OPEN L-101)|W-9]] and [[W-10 37.2's spec write-ups owed since 2 and 3 Nov (OPEN L-32, L-44, L-|W-10]], since 2 and 3 Nov.
- **Parallel numbering collides.** OPEN [[L-32 D4-83 (a fire under no roof in the night's weather fuel by weathe|L-32]]/[[L-35 Distance inside a node|L-35]], [[L-33 Growth to towns fixed nodes, faster edges, reach by place|L-33]]/[[L-40 (Done 3 Nov D4-89 and D4-91 written into economybuildings.md, D4-|L-40]], L-42/[[L-44 D4-93 (one room, one fire a hearth and a ring burn as one; the he|L-44]]/[[L-49 Automated supply-chain and dependence checks for generated worlds|L-49]] and [[L-52 The carrying burden is an incomplete measure|L-52]]/[[L-53 Transport and traders, built out|L-53]]/[[L-60 Should the store line give food in meals and fuel in nights, or l|L-60]] were renumbered on 1 and 2 Nov
  (e0e32ae, 79bfc4a, fea4b8b, 9083557) before the blocks were agreed (12c261e). A row renumbered twice is easy to lose a link to.
